## Reader job and answer boundary

A Louisiana owner finds the Office of Mineral Resources (OMR) or SONRIS and asks whether its state mineral-lease files prove the owner's private mineral lease or interest. A useful answer would distinguish the **state's lease/ownership files** from the **parish conveyance-record office**, then give a research-only next step: identify the parish and exact property/party clues, retain the actual instrument and source locator, and ask the relevant parish recorder or qualified Louisiana title professional what records and limitations apply. Do not promise that any portal or index establishes private title, lease validity, payment rights, or a complete chain of ownership.
