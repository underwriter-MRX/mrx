## Reader job and answer boundary

- Proposed exact title: **Can Pennsylvania DEP Production Data Verify My Royalty Check?**
- Proposed slug: `can-pennsylvania-dep-production-data-verify-my-royalty-check`
- Primary question: A Pennsylvania royalty owner sees a public DEP well-production result and a different volume or payment on a private royalty statement. What can each record actually establish?
- Primary pillar: `/learning-center/oil-and-gas-royalties/`. The article would answer the Pennsylvania *DEP reporting-source and cadence* distinction, not become a generic royalty-statement decoder, payment audit, lease-interpretation guide, or valuation page.
- Direct answer to test in manuscript: **No.** DEP's public production extract is operator-reported well data useful for identifying a well, reporting period, and reported volume; it is not an owner-level royalty-payment ledger. DEP says it does not audit private royalty payments or regulate lease terms. A mismatch is a question to reconcile with the payor's actual statement, lease, division order, and supporting records—not proof of underpayment, ownership, or value.
- Useful next action: Preserve the exact DEP export/reporting period and well or permit identifier alongside the private statement's well/property identifier, production/sales period, product, gross/owner volume and adjustments. Ask the payor for the bridge where identifiers, periods or allocations differ. Keep private documents out of public search and seek a qualified Pennsylvania professional for a disputed lease or payment; the MRX royalty pillar remains a no-contact research path.
