The source-safe provisional answer is: **`RI` commonly abbreviates “royalty interest” in Texas oil-and-gas records, and a Ward CAD detail displays it as an interest-type label separate from the decimal. Neither label alone proves a specific reader's title or payable share.** Any stronger statement about Ward's authoritative data dictionary, exact ownership, computation, or payment must wait for the issuer and controlling private records.
