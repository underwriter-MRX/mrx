## Reader job and pillar

First-party Search Atlas GSC keyword table for the MRX domain, Global scope, 2026-08-01 through 2026-09-23, showed the query “how does wyoming determine oil and gas royalty rates” with 2 impressions and no reported click value. This is weak demand evidence, not proof of the searcher's exact intent. A potentially useful answer for the **oil-and-gas-royalties** pillar is narrower: **Does a Wyoming state or federal public-lease royalty rate set the rate in my private oil-and-gas lease?** The answer must first identify the mineral lessor and the governing lease; it must not imply that a public rate establishes a private owner's contract, decimal, payment, or title.

## Primary-source boundary

- [Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments, Mineral Leasing](https://lands.wyo.gov/trust-land-management/mineral-leasing) is explicitly about **state lands** and offers state oil-and-gas lease, map, and royalty-reporting routes. It does not describe an individual private owner's lease.
- [BLM Wyoming Oil and Gas](https://www.blm.gov/programs/energy-and-minerals/oil-and-gas/about/wyoming) describes BLM-administered **federal** oil-and-gas leases. Its public-lease program is not a private-lease rate schedule.
- [BLM, 2018 Comparative Analysis of the Federal Oil and Gas Fiscal Systems Onshore Report, Wyoming section, PDF page 215](https://www.blm.gov/sites/blm.gov/files/docs/2022-01/2018%20Comparative%20Analysis%20of%20the%20Federal%20Oil%20and%20Gas%20Fiscal%20Systems%20Onshore%20Report_0.pdf) distinguishes private, state, and federal mineral-estate royalty regimes and states that private-land royalties are negotiable and determined by lease terms. This is historical context, **not** a current private-rate benchmark or current public-rate schedule; do not copy its 2018 rate examples into owner advice.
- [BLM Wyoming Federal Mineral Estate layer metadata](https://gis.blm.gov/wyarcgis/rest/services/Lands/BLM_WY_FederalMineralEstate/FeatureServer) expressly says it does not depict private mineral ownership patterns. A map classification cannot establish which lease governs a reader's interest.
