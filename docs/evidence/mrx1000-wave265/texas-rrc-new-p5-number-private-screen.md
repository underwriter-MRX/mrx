## Reader job and pillar

Possible exact question: **Why Does a Texas Operator Have a New RRC P-5 Number After a Name Change?** The reader sees two Texas RRC operator names or numbers and wants to know whether the difference can be an agency-registration event rather than evidence by itself of a new private royalty payor, a property transfer, or a merger. The likely hub is the title/lease/ownership pillar, with a narrow cross-link to the royalty pillar only for the operator-versus-payor distinction. This must remain a field-specific RRC answer, not a generic operator-name log or a corporate-succession opinion.

## Current primary-source boundary

- The RRC's [Section A P-5 Organization Report and Financial Assurance PDF](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/media/j3ahbj2z/section_a.pdf), revised **July 2021**, p. 11 (PDF page index 10), expressly says that a P-5 number assigned to an initial filing cannot be transferred to a different organization name. In the adjacent FAQ it describes a new-name P-5 showing the previous name and operator number and, where applicable, P-4 property-transfer filings. This is official **dated guidance**, not confirmation that every 2026 online control or filing procedure is unchanged. Avoid a universal current filing instruction until checked against the active RRC process.
- The RRC [Research Queries directory](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) and [P-5 Organization Query About](https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/help/P5Organization_About.html) identify the organization query and its displayed name/number/status fields. The directory warns that the online datasets are informational and non-authoritative, without legal force or effect. An observed pair of results is a locator, not a determination of entity identity, merger, well ownership, lease effect, or payment obligation.
- Texas SOS [Form 622 merger instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions/622.shtml) identify parties and surviving/new organizations for a **filed combination merger** and discuss effective dates. These instructions do not mean every name difference represents a merger, that every corporate event uses Form 622, or that an SOS filing establishes a private mineral owner's entitlement. The SOS [Copies and Certificates](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/copies.shtml) page offers official record-copy channels that may carry fees. Do not order a copy or incur a fee without the owner's exact monetary authorization.
- The RRC's [RRC Lonestar page](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/rrc-lonestar/) describes current electronic-service changes. Reconcile it with the older 2021 P-5 PDF before writing a present-tense procedure. Do not imply a live RRC query or SOS purchase was performed in this screen.
