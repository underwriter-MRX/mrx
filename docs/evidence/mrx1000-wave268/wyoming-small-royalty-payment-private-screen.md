## Current primary source and claim boundary

- [Wyoming Legislature, current Title 30, W.S. 30-5-301(a)-(c)](https://www.wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title30.pdf#page=124), directly read 2026-09-26; downloaded response SHA-256 `3a105e55d45a5ab5942de0ef7dbbfcbec747bf41e2dc71ce29ab425014d98616`. The section addresses proceeds from Wyoming oil/gas wells for people legally entitled to payment. Subsection (a) states default first/subsequent timing and expressly allows other periods or arrangements in a valid contract. Subsection (b)(i) provides annual remittance for up to twelve months of accumulated proceeds when the total owed is $100 or less. Subsection (b)(ii) separately addresses a payee's written request when accumulated proceeds are at least $25, with a 60-day remittance period following receipt; (b)(iii)-(iv) add cessation-of-production and payor-responsibility boundaries. Subsection (c) addresses an operator/purchaser payment arrangement. Do not flatten these provisions into “all Wyoming checks are monthly,” “every balance under $100 may be withheld indefinitely,” or advice that a particular reader qualifies for a written-request remedy.
- [W.S. 30-5-302](https://www.wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title30.pdf#page=125) separately addresses when payment cannot be made and an escrow process; it is not evidence that any given missing check was escrowed. The Treasurer's [unclaimed-property page](https://statetreasurer.wyo.gov/unclaimed-property/) is another administrative route, not an automatic explanation for a missing payment. A 2026 Treasurer [reporting-change letter](https://statetreasurer.wyo.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Reporting-changes-for-Royalties.pdf) changes **holder** procedures starting with the 2027 reporting period; do not present it as a current owner's payment deadline or infer a particular payor's reporting status. The older [Mineral Proceeds holder PDF](https://statetreasurer.wyo.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Property-Specific-Mineral-Proceeds.pdf) describes a prior ongoing-payment process and must not override the newer letter.
