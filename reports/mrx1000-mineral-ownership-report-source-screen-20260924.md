The official archive is an example of what one report disclosed, not a template or authority for every report. A report may have no supporting instrument list, different interest definitions, or an express limitation. Missing or conflicting evidence should remain unresolved and be routed to a qualified Texas title professional rather than reconciled by guesswork.
