## Reader job and evidence boundary

The candidate question is: **What should a mineral-rights owner do when a proposed closing or settlement statement shows less than the original written offer?** The potential deliverable is a staged reconciliation register: original offer; executed purchase agreement and amendments; proposed statement; and closing instructions or, after funding, independently verified payment evidence. First compare the offer with the executed agreement, then the agreement with the proposed statement, and only after payment compare the final statement with funds received. That staging prevents an opening-offer difference from being mislabeled as a closing-statement deduction. Within the proper stage, separate a changed interest quantity, changed unit-price basis, contract-defined adjustment, cost or holdback, and unexplained difference without deciding whether any item is legally permitted, fair, owed, taxable, or safe to sign.

This is a narrower stage-of-transaction task under `/offer-review/`, not another generic fair-offer or mineral-value guide. The useful next action would be to request a written, line-by-line explanation and have the actual documents reviewed by an appropriate qualified professional before relying on an unexplained figure. A research-only reader should retain the option to compare documents privately rather than be forced into contact or booking.
