## Reader job and proposed answer boundary

An heir sees North Dakota's *Affidavit for Collection of Personal Property of the Decedent* (Form 1) and wants to know whether it is a shortcut to transfer an inherited **mineral interest**. The useful narrow answer is that North Dakota Courts describes Form 1 as a personal-property collection route for an estate with **no real property in the probate estate**. Its informal-administration inventory illustration lists oil or mineral rights as real-property examples, and its guidance sends estates including probate real property to a personal representative path.
