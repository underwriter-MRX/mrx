## Primary-source boundaries

| Proposition for possible future copy | Official source, read 2026-09-23 | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ohio county recorders keep official records that include deeds and leases/memoranda presented for recording. | [Ohio Revised Code §317.08](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-317.08), divisions (A)(1) and (A)(25), effective June 16, 2026. | A record search is evidence gathering, not a title opinion. An index hit or absence cannot alone decide an owner's interest. |
| The law requires county-recorder websites to make electronic indexes and versions of instruments recorded on or after January 1, 1980 available, subject to stated exceptions. | [Ohio Revised Code §317.13(F)](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-317.13). | Do not imply every historical instrument is online or that every county interface is identical. Do not instruct the reader to record a curative instrument. |
| The ODNR Well Information search exposes county, township and operator-name inputs and explicitly says only active wells are included. | [ODNR Ohio Oil & Gas Well Database — Well Information](https://apps.ohiodnr.gov/oilgas/rbdmsreports/ER_WellInformation.aspx), Search Parameters and note. | A missing result is not proof of no historic well, no production, no lease, or no mineral interest. An operator listed for a well is not automatically this owner's payor. |
| ODNR's database offers multiple oil-and-gas reports besides that active-well search. | [ODNR Ohio Oil & Gas Well Database](https://apps.ohiodnr.gov/oilgas/RBDMSReports/default.aspx), report navigation. | Do not describe the active-well search as the entire Ohio oil-and-gas record system. |

Do not use ODNR water-well results for an oil-and-gas title question. Do not treat a change-of-**well**-owner form as a mineral-owner transfer route. If copy discusses specific Ohio deed, lease, probate, dormant-mineral, or title doctrines, it needs a new current primary-source and compliance review; this screen does not establish those legal conclusions.
