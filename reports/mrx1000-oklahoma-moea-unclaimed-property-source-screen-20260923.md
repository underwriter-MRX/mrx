## Owner job and answer boundary

Candidate question: An Oklahoma mineral owner or heir suspects an old pooling bonus or royalty was not received. Should the owner search the Oklahoma Corporation Commission's Mineral Owners Escrow Account (MOEA) record, the State Treasurer's general unclaimed-property route, or both?

The only potentially distinct answer object is a **two-route search and handoff register**: reason to inspect each issuer's route, exact owner-name variant, search date and final official URL, public result and controlled evidence reference, whether a pooling order or operator record is available, and the agency/holder next-step question. The result labels must be `potential record`, `no result displayed`, or `unverified`, not `owned`, `owed`, `claim approved`, or `title proved`. An heir's real identity, account, deed, claim number, banking and probate documents belong only in the authenticated official process or with a qualified adviser, never in a public worksheet.

Do **not** portray MOEA and the Treasurer as two independent pots of money. The sources describe a custody/reporting relationship; a single underlying payment may be represented in more than one state workflow. This would not be a general missing-royalty, decimal, heirship, title, payment-delay, or investment article. A public search result cannot prove entitlement, and a no-result screen cannot prove that no payment exists.
