## Use the five source-control states literally

### Source-recorded

`Source-recorded` means the records-page relationship, exact URL, displayed document identity, page count, heading, page locator, broad category, date, contractor context, or hash was captured from the retrieved source. It says nothing about applicability, appraisal accuracy, tax treatment, value, or an owner result.

### Not-displayed

`Not-displayed` means a defined item was not found in the specific retrieved source during the recorded review. It does not prove that the item never existed, should appear, is required, or has legal or appraisal significance.

### Source-conflict

`Source-conflict` means the live records page, linked PDF, retained official copy, or other official source displays a different URL, identity, period, page count, heading, page locator, date, contractor attribution, category, or file hash. Preserve each source separately. Do not merge versions or transfer private facts between them.

### Controlled-record-stop

Use `controlled-record-stop` when the next step would expose or require an owner, account, property, lease, regulatory, division-of-interest, production, price, expense, discount-rate, model, notice, appraisal, protest, tax, communication, submission, or transaction record. The label identifies a boundary. It does not authorize access.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before applicability, ownership, interest-type, property or account classification, data selection, method selection, model input, calculation, appraisal treatment, notice review, protest strategy, tax position, legal interpretation, valuation, representation, contact, submission, payment, or sale advice.

The public workflow ends at either stop. It does not open credentials, collect private data, inspect evidence, perform a calculation, contact an office, draft a protest, submit a record, make a payment, or promise a result.

## Handle source drift without merging copies

Source drift can appear as a changed records-page link, filename, period, page count, section title, page locator, displayed date, contractor attribution, category, or hash. When a retained official copy differs from the current source:

1. preserve the live records-page URL and exact current linked PDF URL;
2. preserve the retained official URL or controlled-copy reference;
3. record retrieval date, time, and time zone for each source;
4. record each file's page count and approved hash or pointer;
5. describe the exact displayed difference without normalizing it;
6. mark `source-conflict`; and
7. stop before merging sections, copying private facts, choosing a controlling version, or applying either source.

A newer timestamp does not authorize this page to decide legal or appraisal priority. A matching title does not prove that page locations, details, or effects are unchanged. A different hash proves only that the bytes differ; it does not explain why.
