## Keep each measure inside its official purpose

The [Texas Comptroller Property Tax Data Reports and Surveys page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/reports/) states that the Appraisal District Ratio Study measures the uniformity and median level of appraisals within major property categories. It also says the published reports include median levels of appraisal, coefficient of dispersion, and other statistical measures.

That program-level description is useful for source scope. It does not authorize a property-level conclusion. Keep the following boundaries visible:

- `Number of Ratios` is a displayed study field, not an owner count, account count, parcel count, or production count in this article.
- `CAD Reported Appraisal Value` is a displayed district-summary value, not the value of an owner's minerals, a sale estimate, or an offer.
- `Median Level of Appraisal` is a displayed ratio-study measure, not a property appraisal and not a recommendation.
- `Coefficient of Dispersion` is a displayed statistical measure, not a grade assigned by MRX.
- The two within-median percentages are displayed measures, not probability statements about a particular property.
- `Price-Related Differential` is a displayed ratio-study measure, not a mineral-price differential, commodity-price forecast, royalty adjustment, or transaction term.

Do not add a color grade, pass-fail marker, score, rank, or interpretation to any field. Do not compare the row with another district, year, category, source, appraisal, offer, or sale. The source-control job ends after accurate transcription, official context, limitations, and retrieval provenance.
