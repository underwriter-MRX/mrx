## Use the five document-control states literally

### Source-recorded

`Source-recorded` means the official publisher-page relationship, displayed label, exact endpoint, endpoint family, retrieval date, HTTP status, content type, or content length was captured during the recorded review. It says nothing about the body, records, equivalence, completeness, accuracy, currency, legal status, appraisal, tax, value, or owner result.

### Not-displayed

`Not-displayed` means a defined label or relationship was not found on the specific publisher page during the recorded review. It does not prove that an endpoint never existed, was removed intentionally, should appear, is required, or has legal, appraisal, tax, or valuation significance.

### Source-conflict

`Source-conflict` means a later publisher page or response displays a different label, endpoint, endpoint family, status, content type, or content length. Preserve each observation separately. Do not merge endpoints, infer why a response changed, choose a controlling file, or transfer content assumptions among formats.

### Controlled-record-stop

Use `controlled-record-stop` when the next step would consume a file body; inspect a page, header, row, field, cell, or archive member; access personal or private data; search a property or account; match another source; or expose record content. The label identifies a boundary. It does not authorize access.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before schema comparison, equivalence testing, completeness or accuracy review, legal-certification analysis, property or account interpretation, appraisal judgment, protest strategy, tax position, legal interpretation, valuation, representation, contact, submission, payment, offer analysis, or sale advice.

The public workflow ends at either stop. It does not open credentials, access a file body, collect data, search an account, contact an office, upload a record, file a submission, make a payment, negotiate, or promise a result.

A later review may find a changed label, href, filename, endpoint family, status, content type, content length, section heading, or displayed year. Treat that as source drift rather than an instruction to replace evidence silently.

1. preserve the live publisher-page URL and retrieval time;
