The current **Culberson CAD certified mineral roll ZIP** is linked from the official district Data and Records page. A source-control record should preserve that publisher-page relationship and the exact linked archive before it records even generic archive metadata.

That is the limit of this public job. The record can identify the complete downloaded container and inventory its central directory without extracting or opening a member. It cannot expose or interpret any row.

Use only `source-recorded`, `not-displayed`, `source-conflict`, `controlled-record-stop`, or `authorized-human-stop`. These labels describe source-control status. They do not mean accurate, complete, applicable, current for a later retrieval, appraised, taxable, protestable, legally sufficient, accepted, valuable, or controlling.

It supplies the current display context and link relationship.

A retained ZIP without the publisher-page relationship does not by itself show that the district still presents that file under the same label.

## Do not extract, preview, or sample

The public record ends at the archive boundary. Do not extract a member to a temporary directory. Do not open a PDF, text file, or CSV. Do not display a first row, header, sample, count, summary, thumbnail, preview, or search result. Do not transform a member to another format.

Keep these distinctions literal:

| Permitted container record | Prohibited content action |
| ---- | ---- |
| Exact complete-file URL | Opening a member URL or copied file |
| Complete-file bytes and hash | Hashing an extracted row or displaying member content |
| Generic member name and extension | Reading a header, field layout, page, line, or cell |
| Aggregate uncompressed bytes | Calculating row counts or content statistics |
| Directory and encryption flags | Bypassing controls or testing private-data access |
| Retrieval timestamp | Treating the archive as current for a later date without rechecking |

A temptation to inspect one harmless-looking header is still member-content access. A request for one sample owner, one account, or one property is row-data access. Both require a stop outside this public artifact.

## Preserve source drift instead of normalizing it

The publisher may replace the ZIP while retaining a similar display label, change the link target, revise a filename, or publish another year. Never overwrite the earlier source record silently.

For each retrieval, preserve:

1. Publisher-page URL and displayed link label.
2. Exact final ZIP URL after redirects.
3. Retrieval date, time, time zone, and method.
4. HTTP status and MIME.
5. Complete compressed bytes and SHA-256.
6. Generic central-directory member count, names, extensions, aggregate uncompressed bytes, directory count, and encryption flags.
7. Source limitation and document-control state.

If any item differs, create a new observation and mark `source-conflict` until an authorized process resolves which copy belongs in its controlled context. Do not merge member lists, choose a controlling version, or infer what changed inside a member.

## Use the five source-control states literally

### Source-recorded

`Source-recorded` means the official publisher relationship, exact archive URL, retrieval result, complete-file identity, or permitted central-directory metadata was captured from the retrieved source. It says nothing about row accuracy, completeness, appraisal correctness, tax treatment, legal effect, value, or an owner result.

### Not-displayed

`Not-displayed` means the permitted publisher-page or container metadata does not show the expected item. It does not mean the item does not exist inside a member, in another system, or in another official record.

### Source-conflict

`Source-conflict` means two preserved source observations differ in URL, label, hash, bytes, MIME, member inventory, or another permitted metadata field. Preserve both observations. Do not harmonize them or select a controlling copy through this article.

### Controlled-record-stop

`Controlled-record-stop` applies before any member extraction, member-content access, row-data access, private-data handling, matching, enrichment, or evidence review. The public source-control record ends there.

### Authorized-human-stop

`Authorized-human-stop` applies when the question asks what a row means, whether an appraisal is correct, which law applies, whether to protest, what tax is due, what minerals are worth, or what transaction action to take. Those questions require a separate authorized process and may require qualified appraisal, legal, tax, engineering, or transaction professionals.

## Keep the recorded labels narrow

The terms `certified`, `mineral`, `appraisal roll`, and `open records` appear in the publisher or file naming. This article preserves those labels to identify the source. It does not independently certify the archive, determine who may use a member, declare every row public, or interpret disclosure obligations.

Likewise, the existence of PDF, TXT, and CSV members does not establish that they contain identical information, compatible fields, complete records, or current facts. Their extensions are generic central-directory observations only.

## Apply the final stop check

Before saving a source-control record, ask:

- Did I preserve the official publisher page and exact archive URL separately?
- Did I record only complete-file identity and generic central-directory metadata?
- Did I avoid extracting, opening, reading, searching, indexing, transforming, copying, displaying, or summarizing a member?
- Did I avoid every owner, address, account, property, legal-description, lease, operator, regulatory, interest, production, appraisal, tax, protest, notice, contact, and row value?
- Did I avoid matching, enrichment, analytics, outreach, appraisal, tax, protest, legal, valuation, and sale conclusions?
- Did I preserve source drift rather than normalizing it?
- Did I use only one of the five permitted source-control states?

If any answer is no, stop before the record leaves the controlled process. This page documents a public source container. It is not a row-access tool, ownership lookup, appraisal review, protest plan, tax calculation, legal interpretation, valuation, or sale recommendation.
