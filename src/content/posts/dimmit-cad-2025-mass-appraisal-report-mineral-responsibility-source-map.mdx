## Page 1 establishes the report context

[PDF page 1](https://dimmit-cad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-Mass-Report.pdf#page=1) identifies the issuer and title as the Dimmit Central Appraisal District 2025 Mass Appraisal Report. Under `Scope of Responsibility`, it describes the report as a publication intended to help citizens and taxpayers understand district responsibilities and activities.

The page also describes the appraisal district as responsible for local property-tax appraisal and exemption administration for taxing units in the county. Preserve that as a district report statement. Do not convert it into a property-specific appraisal, tax calculation, title opinion, offer valuation, or representation that MRX acts for the district.

## Page 2 separates annual review, staff duties, and contracted work

[PDF page 2](https://dimmit-cad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-Mass-Report.pdf#page=2) contains two separate responsibility statements that should remain separate:

1. It says personal property, industrial property, complex commercial property, utility property, and mineral-property values are reviewed or reappraised every year.
2. It says the district contracts with Capitol Appraisal Group for appraisals of industrial and mineral properties and industry-related business personal accounts.

The same page describes the chief appraiser's overall planning and control role and lists district staff and support functions. Those details provide an organizational boundary. They do not prove that the named firm performed work on a particular mineral account, identify a current engagement term, describe a contractor method, validate an appraisal, or establish authority or legal effect.

## Page 3 gives a combined inventory, not a mineral count

[PDF page 3](https://dimmit-cad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-Mass-Report.pdf#page=3) says the district is responsible for establishing and maintaining approximately 80,994 real and personal property accounts covering Dimmit County. The next sentence says the data includes property characteristics, ownership, and exemption information and that the account inventory includes mineral and industrial accounts.

The approximate total is district-wide and combined. It is not a mineral-account count, industrial-account count, owner count, tract count, lease count, well count, producing-property count, appraisal count, offer count, or transaction count. The page does not disclose how the total is divided among property categories and does not establish that the number is current after the report period.
