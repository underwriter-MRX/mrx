## Keep every measure inside the official program scope

The [Texas Comptroller Property Tax Data Reports and Surveys page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/reports/) states that the Appraisal District Ratio Study measures uniformity and median appraisal levels within major property categories. It says published reports include median levels of appraisal, coefficient of dispersion, and other statistical measures.

That purpose statement supplies program context, not an owner-specific conclusion. Keep these boundaries visible:

- `Number of Ratios` is a displayed study field, not an owner, account, parcel, well, lease, or production count in this article.
- `CAD Reported Appraisal Value` is a district-summary field, not an owner's mineral value, a market estimate, an offer, or a sale price.
- `Median Level of Appraisal` is a displayed ratio-study measure, not a property appraisal or recommendation.
- `Coefficient of Dispersion` is a displayed statistical measure, not a grade assigned by MRX.
- The two within-median percentages are displayed measures, not probability statements about a property.
- `Price-Related Differential` is a ratio-study field, not a mineral-price differential, commodity forecast, royalty adjustment, or deal term.

Do not add a color grade, pass-fail marker, score, benchmark, or interpretation. Do not compare this row with another district, year, category, source, appraisal, offer, or sale. The job ends after accurate transcription, official context, source limitations, and retrieval provenance.
