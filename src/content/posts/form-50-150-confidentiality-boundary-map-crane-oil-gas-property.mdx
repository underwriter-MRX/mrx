> **Blank-source and controlled-record boundary.** This page maps only the current official Texas Comptroller Form 50-150 source, its displayed identity, four section headings, broad blank categories, Important Information subjects, official-source relationships, retrieval provenance, and the line between reusable public source facts and controlled owner records. It does not enter property or party data, review evidence, decide applicability or filer authority, determine an interest type or value, calculate timing, interpret confidentiality or disclosure, complete or sign the form, contact an office, submit anything, interpret law, provide tax guidance, or prove filing, receipt, acceptance, appraisal treatment, tax treatment, confidentiality, or another result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Form 50-150 Crane oil and gas property** boundary map should answer one limited document-control question: what does the current official blank source display, and which owner-specific records must remain outside a reusable public artifact? The map preserves source identity and broad categories. It never becomes a rendition, a property worksheet, a value estimate, an evidence packet, a filing checklist, or a recommendation to use this form.

Use only `source-recorded`, `not-displayed`, `source-conflict`, `controlled-record-stop`, or `authorized-human-stop`. These labels describe source-control status. They do not mean applicable, authorized, owned, accurate, sufficient, timely, complete, confidential, nondisclosable, signed, notarized, filed, received, accepted, or effective.

## Record the displayed confidentiality treatment literally

The retrieved source visibly displays `CONFIDENTIAL`. Record that exact observation with a page locator and the source hash or controlled-copy reference. Do not expand the observation into a promise about any particular record.

The safe statement is: `The retrieved blank source displays CONFIDENTIAL.` Unsafe transformations include claims that every field is private, that a completed rendition can never be disclosed, that every attachment receives the same treatment, or that an owner can rely on this map to decide what to provide. Those conclusions require current governing-source review and owner-specific professional judgment outside this public artifact.

Keep three concepts separate:

| Concept | What the map may record | What the map must not decide |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Source display | Exact visible `CONFIDENTIAL` treatment and locator | The legal effect of the display |
| Owner record | Category exists and requires controlled handling | Whether a particular value or document is protected |
| Disclosure question | An unresolved question triggered a stop | Whether, when, or to whom a record may be disclosed |

If a person asks whether a specific value, spreadsheet, attachment, appraisal record, or completed source will remain confidential, mark `authorized-human-stop`. Do not answer from the title, a label, a prior filing, or this page.

## Map the four displayed sections without collecting values

The blank PDF displays four numbered sections. Preserve the headings and broad category boundaries, not completed values.

### Section 1: Property Owner Information

Section 1 is the owner-information zone. The public map may record that the blank source has categories for owner identity and contact information. It must not contain an owner name, address, phone number, email address, account identifier, or another owner-specific value.

The existence of an owner-information category does not establish who owns a mineral or royalty interest, who should appear on a rendition, or whether a person is authorized to act. An ownership or identity question is not resolved by a blank field label.

### Section 2: Party Filing Report

Section 2 is the filing-party zone. The public map may record that the source displays party identity, role, and related filing-subject categories. It must not select a filer role, determine authority, affirm a prior-year statement, identify a secured party, or convert a displayed option into a recommendation.

If authority depends on an appointment, relationship, ownership record, prior rendition, or another private fact, stop before reviewing or summarizing that evidence in the public artifact. Route the question to an authorized person using an approved controlled system.

### Section 3: Real Property Information

Section 3 is the property-detail zone. The retrieved blank source displays broad categories associated with property identity, legal description, regulatory reference, rendered interest type, owner estimate, and jurisdictions. The public map may preserve those category names and locators. It must not contain an actual property name, description, identifier, interest selection, estimate, jurisdiction entry, account fact, or valuation statement.

Do not infer that a displayed category applies to a particular mineral, royalty, leasehold, working, overriding, or other interest. Do not estimate value, reconcile ownership, translate a legal description, select jurisdictions, or determine which regulatory identifier belongs in a record. The blank structure is not evidence of a particular property fact.

### Section 4: Affirmation and Signature

Section 4 is the affirmation and execution zone. The public map may record that the source includes affirmation, signature, date, and notary-related categories. It must not include a signature, date, notary fact, identity credential, affirmation answer, or instruction to execute the source.

A displayed signature block does not establish who may sign, what representation is appropriate, whether notarization is required in a specific circumstance, or whether a completed source is valid. Stop before completion, execution, notarization, or submission.

## Keep spreadsheets and attachments in the controlled lane

The source structure contemplates property information that may be represented through rows, schedules, spreadsheets, attachments, or supporting records. The public map may record that a spreadsheet or attachment category exists and where it appears. It must not expose a filename, cell value, row count tied to an owner, property list, legal description, account number, jurisdiction, interest selection, estimate, signature, or supporting document.

Use this split:

| Public source map | Controlled owner record |
| ---- | ---- |

| Section heading and broad blank category | Owner, party, account, property, jurisdiction, interest, or value entry |

A separate authorized process must establish purpose, role, minimum necessary data, recipient, system, channel, retention, and professional-review needs before a controlled record is opened or created.

A source map may record that those subjects are displayed and preserve a page locator. It must not convert the text into owner-specific directions.

## Use the five document-control states literally

### Source-recorded

`Source-recorded` means an exact displayed identity, heading, category, URL, revision, page count, treatment, subject, or source relationship was captured with a locator. It says nothing about an owner's facts, applicability, authority, completion, confidentiality, or result.

### Not-displayed

`Not-displayed` means a defined item was not found in the specific retrieved source during the recorded review. It does not prove the item never existed, should appear, controls another record, or has legal significance.

### Source-conflict

`Source-conflict` means retained official sources display different form numbers, revision lines, page counts, section headings, category labels, treatment labels, URLs, or other defined facts. Preserve each source reference, retrieval time, hash or controlled-copy pointer, and exact observation. Do not merge versions or transfer values.

### Controlled-record-stop

Use `controlled-record-stop` when the next step would expose or require an owner, party, account, contact, jurisdiction, property, regulatory, interest, value, signature, date, notary, spreadsheet, attachment, communication, filing, or outcome record. The label identifies a boundary. It does not grant access or establish a lawful purpose.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before applicability, filer-role, authority, ownership, interest-type, value, timing, extension, confidentiality, disclosure, terminated-exemption, penalty, form-choice, destination, completion, execution, notarization, contact, upload, submission, legal, tax, appraisal, valuation, representation, or strategy decisions.

The public workflow ends at either stop. It does not open credentials, collect private data, inspect evidence, fill a field, choose an office, calculate a date, draft a submission, or transmit anything.

## Handle source drift without merging versions

Source-control drift can appear as a changed revision line, page count, treatment label, section heading, field category, Important Information subject, filename, or URL. When a retained official copy differs from the current source:

1. preserve both exact URLs or controlled-copy references;
2. record retrieval date, time, and time zone for each;
3. record the exact displayed difference without normalizing it;
4. attach a hash or authorized pointer where policy permits;
5. mark `source-conflict`; and
6. stop before copying private values, interpreting the change, or choosing a version.

A later timestamp does not authorize this page to declare legal priority. A matching title does not prove that every category or instruction is unchanged. A different file hash does not explain why the source changed. Preserve evidence and route the question to an authorized human.
