> **Blank-source boundary.** This page inventories only the current official Texas Comptroller Form 50-171 source, its displayed identity, five printed step headings, blank field and attachment categories, official-source relationships, and retrieval provenance. It does not enter owner or property data, inspect a recorded instrument, decide whether the form applies, determine ownership or an interest proportion, select a form or filing destination, calculate timing, complete or sign the form, contact an office, submit anything, interpret law, provide tax guidance, or prove receipt, acceptance, qualification, separate taxation, or a tax result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Form 50-171 Crane mineral interest** field inventory should answer a narrow document-control question: which blank labels and source facts are displayed in the current official copy? The inventory records source identity and structure. It never becomes a completed request, an ownership worksheet, an evidence review, a filing checklist, or a recommendation to use this form.

Use only `source-recorded`, `not-displayed`, `source-conflict`, or `authorized-human-stop`. These labels describe the state of source-control work. They do not mean applicable, eligible, owned, sufficient, timely, complete, correct, authorized, signed, filed, received, accepted, qualified, separately listed, or effective.

## Establish the controlled blank source

Open the current [Texas Comptroller property-tax forms index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/forms/) and follow the official source path to the [Form 50-171 PDF](https://comptroller.texas.gov/forms/50-171.pdf). Preserve both URLs. The index supplies statewide publisher context; the PDF is the blank evidence object whose displayed labels are being inventoried.

Create a source header before recording any field category:

| Source-control field      | Blank record                                         |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Inventory ID              | Nonidentifying internal reference                    |
| Publisher                 | Organization displayed by the official source        |
| Forms index URL           | Exact statewide index URL                            |
| Document URL              | Exact Form 50-171 PDF URL                            |
| Retrieved at              | Date, time, and time zone                            |
| Displayed identity        | Title and form number copied from the source         |
| Displayed revision        | Exact revision line, without interpreting its effect |
| Page count                | Pages in the retrieved controlled copy               |
| Controlled-copy reference | Authorized pointer, not a public owner document      |
| Limitation                | Blank-source field inventory only                    |
| State                     | One of the four allowed document-control labels      |

Do not begin with a completed form, owner file, recorded instrument, appraisal record, tax statement, email attachment, search snippet, third-party upload, or memory. A saved copy may be retained as a comparison source, but it does not silently replace the current official source. If the official copy cannot be retrieved or its identity cannot be confirmed, use `authorized-human-stop`.

## Record the displayed document identity literally

The current official PDF identifies itself as **Request for Separate Taxation of an Undivided Interest**, displays form number **50-171**, and shows revision line **04-17/3**. The retrieved source contains two pages and displays **General Instructions** and **Filing Instructions** before its five numbered steps.

Those are source facts, not a determination that a particular Crane mineral interest is an undivided interest within the document’s scope. Likewise, a form title is not proof of ownership, eligibility, authority, qualification, or a tax treatment. Record the exact displayed identity and preserve its locator. Stop before applying it to an owner.

## Inventory the five printed steps as blank categories

The current blank source displays these step headings:

1. **Request of Information**
2. **Property Description and Required Documentation**
3. **Agent’s Name and Address**
4. **List Owner’s Names and Addresses**
5. **Read, Sign and Date**

For every step, create one neutral provenance row. The public row can contain an exact displayed heading, a broad blank category, a page locator, a controlled-copy pointer, a source-control state, and a limitation. It cannot contain an owner value.

| Inventory field      | Permitted blank entry                                                             |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Printed step heading | Exact displayed heading                                                           |
| Displayed category   | Neutral category only                                                             |
| Source locator       | Page and source reference                                                         |
| State                | `source-recorded`, `not-displayed`, `source-conflict`, or `authorized-human-stop` |
| Controlled-copy link | Authorized pointer only                                                           |
| Limitation           | What the recorded category does not establish                                     |

This structure deliberately separates a printed label from the private value that might later be entered by an authorized person. A blank label can be public source evidence. An owner name, mailing address, property description, interest proportion, recorded instrument, agent identity, signature, or date is not part of this reusable public inventory.

### Step 1: request information without owner values

Step 1 displays blank categories for the property owner’s identity and contact information, together with a category for the proportion of the undivided interest. Record only that those categories appear and where they appear. Do not enter a name, phone number, mailing address, percentage, fraction, decimal, or example value.

A displayed proportion field does not establish how an interest should be calculated or expressed. This page does not derive net mineral acres, interpret a deed, resolve a decimal, allocate ownership, or verify the numerator and denominator of any claimed interest. If an actual proportion must be determined, the blank-source inventory ends.

### Step 2: property and attachment categories without evidence review

Step 2 displays a property-description category and a required-documentation subject. The blank source also identifies an attachment category involving a duly executed and recorded instrument of title. Inventory the category and exact source locator. Do not attach, read, summarize, validate, or publish an actual instrument.

That separation matters. The presence of an attachment label does not prove that a retained document is recorded, executed, current, complete, sufficient, authentic, or connected to the person or property at issue. The inventory does not search county records, trace title, resolve legal descriptions, compare grantor and grantee names, calculate interests, or decide what evidence meets a requirement.

A safe public row says only that an attachment category is displayed in the retrieved blank source. Any instrument review is a separate controlled process requiring an authorized purpose, approved access, minimum-necessary handling, and qualified review where appropriate.

### Step 3: agent categories without an authority conclusion

Step 3 displays blank categories for an agent’s name and address. Record the categories, page locator, and source state. Do not identify an agent, infer that an agency relationship exists, create authority, judge supporting documentation, or decide who may complete, sign, contact, or submit.

A blank agent field is not evidence of appointment, consent, representation, or signing authority. If an owner-specific workflow requires an agent decision, use `authorized-human-stop` before any name or contact information is entered.

### Step 4: other-owner categories without building an owner list

Step 4 displays categories for owners’ names and addresses. Record only the category and source locator. Do not populate an owner list, create a fictional example, copy public appraisal data into the template, infer co-ownership, reconcile spelling, determine who should be included, or disclose a mailing address.

Even when a value can be found in a public record, this inventory does not republish it. A later authorized process must define its purpose, lawful basis, recipient, approved system, and minimum-necessary data before private or owner-specific information is handled.

### Step 5: signature categories without execution

Step 5 displays read, sign, and date subjects and a blank execution area. Record the printed category and locator. Do not decide who must read or sign, evaluate a statement, reproduce a signature, enter a date, authenticate identity, witness, notarize, complete, or submit anything.

The presence of a signature area proves no authority, assent, truth, completion, delivery, receipt, acceptance, or legal effect. Execution is outside the field-inventory workflow.

## Keep source facts, private values, and evidence in separate lanes

A reliable inventory distinguishes four evidence objects:

- **Official blank source:** the retrieved Form 50-171 PDF with its URL and controlled-copy reference.
- **Public field inventory:** the displayed identity, headings, blank categories, locators, provenance, limitations, and source-control states recorded on this page.
- **Owner-controlled working copy:** any owner, agent, property, proportion, signature, or date values entered by an authorized person for an approved purpose.
- **Evidence packet:** any recorded instrument, authority document, submission record, receipt, or official result retained through a separate authorized process.

Do not backfill the public inventory with data from the other lanes. Do not infer that a working copy was completed because the blank source exists. Do not infer that evidence is sufficient because the form prints an attachment category. Do not infer that a request was submitted, accepted, or effective because a field was entered.

## Treat the instruction blocks as displayed subjects

The General Instructions and Filing Instructions contain the source’s stated subject matter and reference Texas Tax Code Section 25.11(b). They also display concepts related to an undivided interest, a written request, a chief appraiser, supporting title material, and timing.

The field inventory may record that those subjects appear. It must not convert them into personalized directions about:

- whether a particular Crane mineral interest falls within the form’s scope;
- who owns an interest or what proportion is correct;
- whether a person may act for an owner;
- whether an instrument is executed, recorded, authentic, or sufficient;
- which form, appraisal district, office, person, address, or channel should be used;
- when any period begins, ends, renews, or changes;
- whether a request is complete or timely;
- whether an interest will be listed, appraised, or taxed separately; or
- what legal, appraisal, or tax consequence follows.

Those questions require current official sources, owner-specific facts, authorized handling, and qualified review. A blank-source inventory cannot resolve them.

The official blank form references Section 25.11(b). The current [Texas Tax Code Chapter 25](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.25.htm) is the official statutory source for that reference. Record the relationship without paraphrasing the statute into an owner-specific rule.

| Printed category | The blank source contains ownership, property, proportion, instrument, agent, owner-list, signature, and date categories | Proof sufficiency, qualification, filing effect, appraisal treatment, or tax treatment |

## Use the four document-control states literally

### Source-recorded

`Source-recorded` means an exact displayed identity, heading, blank category, URL, revision, page count, statutory reference, or source relationship was captured with a locator. It says nothing about owner-specific applicability, truth, sufficiency, completion, or result.

### Not-displayed

`Not-displayed` means a defined item was not found in the specific retrieved source during the recorded review. It does not prove that the item never existed, should appear, controls another source, or has legal significance.

### Source-conflict

`Source-conflict` means retained official sources display different form numbers, revision lines, page counts, headings, field labels, attachment categories, URLs, or other defined source facts. Preserve both source references, retrieval times, hashes or controlled-copy pointers, and exact observations. Do not merge versions, transfer values, or select which copy controls.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before private-data entry, ownership or proportion determination, recorded-instrument review, agent-authority review, applicability or qualification decisions, form selection, completion, signature, contact, upload, filing, submission, payment, timing calculation, legal interpretation, tax guidance, appraisal judgment, valuation, representation, or strategy.

The public workflow ends at the stop. It does not calculate a date, fill a field, choose an office, attach proof, open credentials, or transmit anything.

## Handle revision or field conflicts without merging

Source-control drift can appear as a changed revision line, title, page count, instruction heading, step heading, field label, attachment label, or URL. When a retained official copy differs from the current source:

1. preserve both exact URLs or controlled-copy references;
2. record retrieval date, time, and time zone for each;
3. record the exact difference without normalizing it;
4. attach a hash or authorized pointer where policy permits;
5. mark `source-conflict`; and
6. stop before copying private values or choosing a version.

A newer retrieval timestamp does not authorize this page to declare legal priority. A matching title does not prove that every field is unchanged. A different filename does not prove that the underlying document differs. Preserve evidence and route the question to an authorized human.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does source-recorded mean Form 50-171 applies to my mineral interest?

No. It means only that an identified blank-source fact was captured with provenance. It does not decide applicability, ownership, eligibility, qualification, timing, completion, filing, acceptance, or tax treatment.

### Can this inventory tell me whether my deed or other instrument is sufficient?

No. It records only that the blank source displays an attachment category. It does not inspect an instrument, confirm execution or recording, trace title, decide sufficiency, or identify what a particular owner must provide.

### Can the public inventory contain a property description or ownership proportion?

No. Keep owner, agent, property, proportion, instrument, signature, and date values in an authorized controlled system. The reusable public inventory stays blank.

### What if two official copies display different fields or revision lines?

Preserve both sources and mark `source-conflict`. Do not merge them, transfer values, select the controlling version, or continue to completion or submission.

### Does this page prove separate taxation was requested or granted?

No. It proves no form selection, completion, signature, filing, receipt, acceptance, qualification, separate listing, appraisal treatment, or tax result.
