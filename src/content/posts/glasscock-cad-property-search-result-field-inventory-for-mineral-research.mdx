## Treat Market Value and Base Tax Due as labels only

Two displayed phrases can be especially easy to overread: **Market Value** and **Base Tax Due**.

This inventory does not define either phrase, capture an amount, explain an appraisal method, calculate a tax, reconcile a bill, or connect a displayed value to a mineral-rights transaction. In particular:

- Market Value is not treated here as a mineral-rights sale valuation, offer benchmark, expected proceeds, reserve value, or transaction price.
- Base Tax Due is not treated here as a current bill, payoff amount, lien conclusion, liability, closing adjustment, or legal obligation.
- The presence of either label does not prove that a result would contain a value or that any displayed value would answer the reader's question.

If a later job needs an appraisal record, tax statement, title source, offer comparison, or mineral-interest analysis, it needs a separately defined source package and review standard.
