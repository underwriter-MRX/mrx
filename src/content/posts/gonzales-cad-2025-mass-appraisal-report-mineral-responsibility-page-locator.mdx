> **Official-source locator boundary.** This page identifies where one official Gonzales CAD report states responsibility for industrial, utility, and mineral valuation. It does not import the report body; retrieve an owner, parcel, lease, account, or appraisal; replicate a valuation method; verify title or ownership; calculate tax; estimate mineral-rights sale value; compare buyers or offers; recommend a sale; interpret law; or promise an outcome. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The [Gonzales CAD 2025 Mass Appraisal Report](https://www.gonzalescad.org/Forms/GetDocument?fileName=1773844462_2025+Mass+Appraisal+Reprot.pdf&subPath=Data+Records) places **Industrial, Utility and Mineral Valuation** on **printed page 18**, which is **PDF page 22** in the 26-page file. Under **Appraisal Responsibility**, the report attributes the covered work to Pritchard and Abbot Valuation Consultants, Inc. and says the scope includes mineral valuation, including but not limited to oil and gas reserves.

That is the complete answer this locator owns. It identifies a document, section, page pair, and attributed responsibility statement. It does not establish what any Gonzales County mineral interest is worth, who owns it, whether an appraisal is correct, or what a buyer should offer.
