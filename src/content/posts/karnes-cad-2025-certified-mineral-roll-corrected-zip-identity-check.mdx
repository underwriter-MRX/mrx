## Treat the integrity result as non-validating

The complete archive passed `unzip -t`: the tool reported each of the seven members as `OK` and detected no compressed-data error. That is a narrow container-integrity observation.

It does not prove:

- that every expected account or record is present;
- that a field is accurate, current, complete, or legally controlling;
- that an archive member belongs to a named owner or tract;
- that the `Corrected` filename describes which data changed;
- that an appraisal, tax, ownership, title, acreage, production, royalty, value, offer, or transaction conclusion follows.

The archive itself is the source for its container state. The [Karnes CAD homepage](https://karnescad.org/) supports the current public-site identity, not the truth of every archive row.
