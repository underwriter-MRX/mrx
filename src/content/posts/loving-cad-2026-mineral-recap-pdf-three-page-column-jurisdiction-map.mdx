## Preserve the page-to-page row difference

Physical page 1 displays five jurisdiction rows. Physical pages 2 and 3 each display seven. The later pages show two numbered jurisdiction lines as paired rows, but the report itself does not provide an explanatory legend on the reviewed pages.

There is also a visible glyph issue. Page 2’s split-row prefix appears as `M&O`, while the corresponding page 3 prefix visually resembles `M&0`. This article does not silently change the zero-like glyph into a letter, expand the abbreviation, or assert that both displays have the same intended meaning.

Keep at least four fields separate in working notes:

1. physical page number;
2. displayed jurisdiction number;
3. exact visible row prefix and label;
4. the source column group on that page.

Do not merge the paired rows merely because they share a jurisdiction number. A source-preserving worksheet can add a separate analyst note, but it should never replace the displayed text.
