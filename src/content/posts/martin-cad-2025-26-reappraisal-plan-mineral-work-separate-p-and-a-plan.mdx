The [Martin CAD Data and Records page](https://martincad.org/home/DataRecords) separately displays a `2025 - 2026 Reappraisal Plan` link and a `2025 - 2026 MIUP Appraisal Plan` link under Reappraisal Plans. The exact linked [general reappraisal plan](https://martincad.org/Forms/GetDocument?fileName=1726094026_2025-2026+Reappraisal+Plan.pdf&subPath=Data+Records) is an 18-page scanned PDF.

The direct answer is on physical and printed page 15. Under **MINERAL, INDUSTRIAL, UTILITY, AND RELATED PERSONAL PROPERTY**, the general plan says Martin CAD contracts with Pritchard & Abbott for appraisal work in those areas and says a 2025-2026 Pritchard & Abbott reappraisal plan is on file for the areas for which that contractor is responsible. Treat that as a source-attributed handoff to a separate plan. Do not assume that the separately linked MIUP PDF is the exact on-file plan unless Martin CAD explicitly confirms the identity.

## Separate the general plan from the contractor plan

Physical and printed page 4 visibly names a **residential, commercial, rural, and personal property 2025/2026 reappraisal plan** and describes a three-year-cycle framework. That page supplies general-plan scope. It does not supply the separate contractor plan's mineral methodology.

Physical and printed page 15 then introduces a different source lane. The heading includes mineral, industrial, utility, and related personal property. The paragraph says, in substance, that Martin CAD contracts with Pritchard & Abbott for appraisal work in those areas and that a 2025-2026 Pritchard & Abbott plan is on file for the responsible areas.

Record that relationship as `SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED`. The paragraph does not attach a filename, URL, file hash, page count, version, or adoption record for the referenced plan. It also does not identify a property or account. Those absences are part of the evidence boundary, not blanks to fill with inference.
