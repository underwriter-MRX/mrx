## Do not convert parcel count into owner or interest count

The row's column is labeled as a parcel count. The source does not say that the figure equals the number of:

- mineral owners;
- mineral interests;
- royalty interests;
- producing leases;
- wells;
- tax accounts;
- division-order payees; or
- potential mineral-rights transactions.

Those categories can use different units and relationships. One owner can be associated with more than one parcel. One parcel can involve more than one interest or owner. The report row alone does not supply the mapping needed to move between those categories.

Record the literal source unit: `parcel count`. If a separate official source defines a different unit, preserve that source, date, scope, and definition as another claim. Do not merge the two simply because their numbers appear related.

## Do not convert aggregate market value into a sale value or offer

The physical-page-2 figure is the market-value column for the report's aggregate Oil & Gas (G\*) row. It is not the following:

- a per-parcel value;
- a price per net mineral acre;
- a royalty forecast;
- a discounted-cash-flow result;
- a mineral-rights purchase offer;
- a tax bill;
- a closing amount; or
- proof of what any reader owns.

Even the phrase `market value` must remain attached to the report and its stated appraisal purpose. A mineral-rights transaction can involve title, decimal ownership, lease terms, production history, deductions, timing, development assumptions, buyer criteria, and other evidence that this aggregate row does not provide.

If the practical question is about a specific appraisal or tax record, use the current [Texas Comptroller local property-appraisal directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/) to locate the applicable appraisal district or tax office. The directory is routing evidence. It does not interpret the Martin report or provide a transaction opinion.
