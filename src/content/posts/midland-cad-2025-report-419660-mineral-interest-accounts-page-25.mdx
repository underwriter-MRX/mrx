> **Report statement, not an owner or property result.** This guide locates and interprets one statement in an official Midland Central Appraisal District report. It does not identify a mineral owner, account, tract, well, lease, acreage position, title interest, appraisal, tax result, sale value, offer, production result, royalty amount, or contractor-performance finding. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: the official [2025 Midland CAD Mass Appraisal Report](https://midcad.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-Midland-CAD-Mass-Appraisal-Report-Final.pdf) states on **physical PDF page 25** that industrial appraisers are responsible for the valuation of **approximately 419,660 mineral interest accounts**.

Read every part of that sentence. The source says **approximately**, not exactly. Its counted unit is **mineral interest accounts**, not owners, tracts, wells, leases, producing interests, or net mineral acres. The statement appears in a 2025 report and does not label the figure as a live, continuously updated account total.

## Preserve “approximately” rather than silently normalizing the count

The report qualifies the account count with **approximately**. Removing that word would create a stronger claim than the source makes.

The controlled interpretation is therefore:

An approximation can still be useful for locating and understanding a source statement. It cannot support false precision.
