Physical page 8 prints **Thursday, May 4, 2026**, although May 4, 2026 was Monday. Physical page 22, which carries the printed footer **Page 21 of 21**, also prints **May 4, 2026**.

## Mineral scope on physical page 10

Physical page 10, printed Page 10 of 21, lists four approximate account categories. The mineral line is **`Category G(Mineral): Approximately 424,996 accounts`**. The same scope section states that the appraisal firm would:

- appraise mineral and other listed property categories;
- compile appraised values as of January 1 of each tax year;
- handle approved extensions for mineral accounts;
- provide working-interest and royalty-interest value by lease;
- enter necessary data into the district's computer-assisted mass-appraisal system;
- prepare summary appraisal reports; and
- handle informal and formal protests and specified later proceedings.

These are source statements about requested contractor work. They do not establish that the listed services were performed, that a named firm performed them, that every account was included, that a value is accurate, or that an owner received a particular result.

The approximate 424,996 figure is also not interchangeable with the **419,660 mineral interest accounts** statement in Midland CAD's 2025 mass-appraisal report. ## Why the proposal dates remain unresolved

Physical page 4 says the district must receive proposals by **March 30, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. Central Standard Time**. Physical page 8 says they must be received by **2:00 p.m. Central Standard Time on Thursday, May 4, 2026**. A calendar check places May 4, 2026 on Monday, not Thursday. Schedule 8 on physical page 22 repeats **May 4, 2026** for proposal submission.

Those observations create two separate controls:

1. the March 30 versus May 4 date conflict; and
2. the Thursday versus Monday weekday conflict attached to May 4.

This article does not choose a winner, infer a typographical correction, or declare a deadline invalid. The PDF says written addenda may interpret, correct, or change proposal documents. A reader who needs the operative instruction should ask Midland CAD for the controlling solicitation record and any addenda.

## Statutory and classification context

[Texas Tax Code Section 25.01](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.25.htm) says a chief appraiser, with approval of the district's board of directors, may contract with a private appraisal firm to perform appraisal services. It also states that compensation cannot be contingent on the amount of or increase in appraised, assessed, or taxable value.

That statute explains why a district might solicit a private appraisal firm and supplies one contract-control rule. This article does not apply the statute to a bidder, proposal, award, or contract, and it is not legal advice.

The [Texas Comptroller Property Classification Guide](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/docs/96-313.pdf) identifies Category G as real property involving oil and gas, minerals, and other subsurface interests, with G1, G2, and G3 subcategories. That context decodes the RFP's Category G label. It does not validate or recalculate the approximately 424,996 figure.

## A verification checklist before relying on the RFP

Use this sequence if the solicitation matters to a current research question:

1. **Bind the source.** Record the Publications page, PDF URL, retrieval time, bytes, SHA-256, and page count.
2. **Use dual page locators.** Cite the physical PDF page and the printed footer because the counts differ.
3. **Preserve every date string.** Do not normalize March 30 and May 4 into one date.
4. **Record the weekday mismatch.** Keep `Thursday, May 4, 2026` as source text and Monday as the separate calendar observation.
5. **Look for addenda.** Ask Midland CAD whether a written addendum, replacement RFP, board packet, or notice clarified the dates.
6. **Separate milestones from outcomes.** An anticipated award, execution, or start date does not prove the event occurred.
7. **Separate scope from performance.** A scope item does not prove a selected contractor completed it.
8. **Separate approximate counts from account facts.** The Category G statement cannot establish a particular owner's record or value.
9. **Use the correct official channel.** Rely on Midland CAD for current procurement instructions and district record status.
