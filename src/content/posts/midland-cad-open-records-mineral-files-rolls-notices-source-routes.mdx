> **Official routing, not a property result.** This guide records where Midland Central Appraisal District places selected links on its Open Records page. It does not identify a mineral owner, account, property, tract, well, lease, acreage position, title interest, appraisal, tax result, protest outcome, sale value, offer, production result, royalty amount, or legal conclusion. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: Midland CAD's current [Open Records page](https://midcad.org/open-records/) places the **2026 Preliminary Mineral Roll** under **Preliminary Rolls**. It places the **2026 Midland Certified Mineral Roll** and **2026 Mineral Notices** under **Mineral Files**.

That hierarchy matters. The page also has a **Certified Rolls** heading, but the retrieved page does not place the certified mineral roll under that heading. A useful source note should preserve the page's displayed structure instead of silently reorganizing similar-sounding links.

## Preserve the heading hierarchy instead of normalizing it

The visible Open Records page uses several section headings, including **Certified Rolls**, **Preliminary Rolls**, **Mineral Files**, and **Miscellaneous Files**. Similar terms appear in different places, which makes silent cleanup tempting and unsafe.

For example, the certified mineral roll sounds like it could belong under Certified Rolls. On the retrieved page, it does not. The **2026 Midland Certified Mineral Roll** appears under **Mineral Files**. The **2026 Preliminary Mineral Roll** appears under **Preliminary Rolls**. The page also places **2026 Mineral Notices** under **Mineral Files** rather than under a separate Notices heading.

This guide does not assert that one classification is better than another. It records the observed structure so a reader or researcher can reproduce the route. If Midland CAD changes the page later, the current page should be rechecked rather than forcing a new layout into the prior evidence.

The machine-readable [Open Records WordPress record](https://midcad.org/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?slug=open-records&_fields=id,modified_gmt,link,slug,title,content) identifies page ID `5797`, slug `open-records`, title `Open Records`, and `modified_gmt` value `2026-08-17T13:07:37`. That timestamp is WordPress metadata. It is not represented here as a visible publication date, an effective date for every link, or proof that each target file changed at that time.

## A link establishes navigation, not contents or validity

An official-domain link can support a narrow provenance statement: this is the route Midland CAD displayed when the page was retrieved. The link does not, by itself, answer what is inside an archive.

Do not convert a displayed link into any of these conclusions:

| Unsupported conclusion                       | Missing evidence                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A record exists for a named owner            | No archive search, account match, owner-deduplication method, or current district response         |
| A file applies to a particular property      | No account, parcel, legal-description, tract, or property crosswalk                                |
| The archive is complete or current           | No completeness statement, field dictionary, inclusion rule, exclusion rule, or as-of validation   |
| The file proves mineral ownership or title   | No title chain, instrument review, conveyance interpretation, or qualified title determination     |
| A notice was delivered or has a legal effect | No recipient match, delivery evidence, deadline review, protest record, or governing-rule analysis |
| A roll establishes private mineral value     | No transaction interest, lease, production, reserve, timing, risk, or offer evidence               |
| A filename proves appraisal accuracy         | No account-specific appraisal record, method review, supporting data, or error analysis            |

The same rule applies to apparently precise filenames. A target ending in `.zip` or `.7z` identifies the linked path's archive suffix. It does not establish member names, schemas, record counts, data quality, security, completeness, or fitness for a particular question.

## Keep preliminary and certified labels attached to their sources

The words **preliminary** and **certified** are not interchangeable. The page labels one mineral roll preliminary and another certified. This guide preserves those labels and does not reconcile them into one generic "mineral roll."

That distinction still does not explain every operational detail a reader might need. The page label alone does not define:

- the file's effective date or data cutoff;
- the certification scope represented by the file;
- whether a later replacement exists;
- member filenames or field definitions;
- confidential-field treatment;
- account-status meanings;
- correction, supplement, or amendment handling; or
- whether a particular record is present.

Those questions require the target artifact, its documentation, or a current response from the responsible office. They cannot be answered by label similarity.

## The request-form label and PDF title do not match

The Open Records page displays the link text **Open Records Request From**. The linked [one-page PDF](https://midcad.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ORR-Form-request.pdf) is titled **Request for Information** in its visible page content.

This guide preserves both observations. It does not silently change `From` to `Form`, claim the displayed label is intentional, or infer that the PDF is invalid because the strings differ.

The PDF offers two choices: inspect information or receive copies. It asks the requester to list the specific information and number of copies so records may be identified. It also contains blank requester contact fields. Those are form-layout observations, not evidence that responsive records exist, that a request will be granted, what a response will contain, how long it will take, or what cost may apply.

## A reproducible route-first workflow

Use this sequence when the goal is to locate a Midland CAD mineral-related source without overstating it:

1. Open the current official Open Records page.
2. Record the exact visible section heading and link label.
3. Record the target URL and filename suffix without rewriting the label.
4. Record the retrieval date and, when available, the page-record identity.
5. Decide whether the question requires only routing evidence or the actual target artifact.
6. If the artifact is required, preserve its complete byte hash, archive identity, member list, field definitions, and limitations before making a content claim.
7. Keep owner, property, account, title, appraisal, tax, production, royalty, and transaction conclusions outside the route-only step.
8. Use a current official contact route when the page or artifact does not answer the question.

This sequence makes gaps visible. It does not create a substantive result from missing evidence.

## Five questions to ask before relying on a linked file

Before using a roll, notice, or mineral-file link for a specific decision, ask:

1. What exact file, revision, and retrieval date are being used?
2. Was the file downloaded successfully and preserved with a complete hash?
3. What do its fields, status labels, inclusions, exclusions, and effective date mean?
4. Does an identified record actually match the relevant owner, account, property, or interest?
5. What separate evidence is required for title, appraisal, tax, production, royalty, value, or transaction questions?

If one answer is missing, label the gap. Do not fill it with a filename assumption.
