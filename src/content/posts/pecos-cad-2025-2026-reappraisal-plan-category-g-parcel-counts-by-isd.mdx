> **Official-plan table locator, not an owner or certified-roll result.** This article records the labels and figures printed in Pecos County Appraisal District’s 2025-2026 Reappraisal Plan. It does not prove that planned work was completed, that an estimate became a certified count, or that a named owner, well, lease, tract, mineral interest, account, notice, tax bill, value, offer, or transaction is represented. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **physical page 37 prints two separate Category G estimates**. The table places **20,005** under **2025 Buena Vista ISD** and **78,200** under **2026 Fort Stockton ISD** for **G Real: Oil, Gas and Other Minerals**. The table heading says **Estimated Parcel Counts by ISD**.

Keep the year, school district, Category G label, estimate status, and physical-page locator attached to each figure. Do not add the figures as a Pecos County total or convert them into a different unit.

## Direct answer: the two Category G table cells

| Plan year | ISD               | Physical page 37 label              | Printed estimate | What it does not establish                                      |
| --------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2025      | Buena Vista ISD   | G Real: Oil, Gas and Other Minerals | 20,005           | Owners, wells, leases, tracts, interests, completion, or status |
| 2026      | Fort Stockton ISD | G Real: Oil, Gas and Other Minerals | 78,200           | Owners, wells, leases, tracts, interests, completion, or status |

The [official Pecos CAD Data and Records page](https://www.pecoscad.org/home/DataRecords) links the [2025-2026 Reappraisal Plan PDF](https://pecoscad.org/Forms/GetDocument?fileName=1725366925_2025-2026+reappraisal+plan.pdf&subPath=Data+Records). The page-37 cells are plan-table statements. They are not a current account search, certified roll, mineral-interest inventory, or title record.

## Bind the exact source before using a figure

The controlled PDF retrieved for this review is **971,223 bytes** with SHA-256 **c95d84641a0d024c04b9d8ba491e5972085b29a05a9aba4038128d78e8e12d01**. It has 75 physical pages, is unencrypted, and contains no embedded JavaScript. Its metadata records creation on August 8, 2024 and modification on September 2, 2024.

Physical page 1 prints **Approved by Board of Directors August 19, 2024**. That is a literal cover statement, not independent proof of legal validity, later implementation, current status, or controlling effect.

The file controls make a citation reproducible. They do not validate a table estimate or connect it to one owner or property.

## How physical page 2 connects years to school districts

Physical page 2 says all real property in Buena Vista ISD will be reappraised in 2025 and all real property in Fort Stockton ISD will be reappraised in 2026. It schedules Iraan/Sheffield ISD for 2027 and points readers to Exhibit A for the proposed 2025 and 2026 work schedules and category breakdown.

That sequence explains why physical page 37 presents the first two columns as:

- **2025 Buena Vista ISD**; and
- **2026 Fort Stockton ISD**.

The schedule remains plan language. It does not prove that every real or personal property was reappraised, that the figures were later certified, or that the same figures are current.

[Texas Tax Code Chapter 25](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.25.htm) supplies periodic-reappraisal-plan and appraisal-roll context. This article does not interpret an owner’s duty, apply law to an account, determine a deadline, or decide whether Pecos CAD satisfied a requirement.

## Category G and the all-category totals are different rows

Physical page 37 prints more than the Category G row. It also prints an all-category **Totals** row:

| Column                 | Category G estimate | All-category total |
| ---------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------ |
| 2025 Buena Vista ISD   | 20,005              | 28,038             |
| 2026 Fort Stockton ISD | 78,200              | 101,979            |

The all-category totals include the table’s other displayed property categories. They are not alternate Category G figures. This article does not calculate a percentage, ratio, combined total, trend, or explanation from the four cells.

The [Pecos CAD homepage](https://pecoscad.org/) says mineral, utility, and industrial accounts are appraised for the district by Thos. Y. Pickett and Company. That homepage statement identifies a district role. It does not define what one page-37 “parcel” means, confirm the estimates, or establish any owner-level record.

## Keep physical page 40 separate from physical page 37

Physical page 40 begins a separately titled **Oil and Gas Reserves** appraisal-procedures appendix. It states that the contractor annually reappraises producing mineral leases and describes January 1 tax-appraisal context.

That appendix is useful for locating a different part of the plan. It does not say that the page-37 Category G row counts producing mineral leases. It also does not authorize changing “Estimated Parcel Counts by ISD” into wells, leases, tracts, owners, producing properties, mineral interests, or appraisal records.

The safe rule is simple: **use page 37 for the table label and figures; do not borrow a different section’s noun to redefine the table unit**.

## A source-preserving verification sequence

Use this sequence when a Category G figure appears in a memo, spreadsheet, or research note:

1. **Record the official route.** Save the Data and Records page and the PDF URL.
2. **Bind the file.** Record retrieval time, byte size, SHA-256, page count, encryption status, and JavaScript status.
3. **Cite physical page 37.** Preserve the table heading and Category G row wording.
4. **Keep each column intact.** Pair 20,005 with 2025 Buena Vista ISD and 78,200 with 2026 Fort Stockton ISD.
5. **Keep “estimated” visible.** Do not relabel a plan estimate as current, actual, completed, final, or certified.
6. **Do not sum the two figures.** The source does not label their sum as a county total.
7. **Do not change units.** If a later task requires owner, account, well, lease, tract, mineral-interest, or certified-roll counts, retrieve the source that expressly reports that unit.
8. **Verify current status separately.** Use Pecos CAD’s current official records for a live account, notice, certified roll, protest, or tax-appraisal question.
9. **Use title and transaction evidence separately.** An appraisal-plan table cannot prove ownership, title, private value, an offer, or a sale result.
