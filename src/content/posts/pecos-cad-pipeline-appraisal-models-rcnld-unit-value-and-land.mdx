> **Pipeline-model source guide, not a property valuation.** This article records the pipeline model sequence on physical pages 54 and 56 through 59 of Pecos County Appraisal District's 2025-2026 Reappraisal Plan. It does not identify a pipeline owner, establish an easement or right of way, validate a model input, calculate depreciation, allocate system value, determine tax, appraise a mineral interest, guide a protest, or produce a private value or transaction result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **Pecos CAD's plan says every pipeline receives a replacement-cost-new-less-depreciation calculation, while a unit-value model may also be used only when the stated financial data condition is met**. The unit-value result is built for the entire pipeline system, reconciled with the RCNLD indicator, and then may be allocated among system components. The plan treats land through a separate responsibility and reduction step.

Those are descriptions of a local ad valorem appraisal process. They are not a private mineral valuation method and do not establish the value of a pipeline segment, easement, surface estate, mineral interest, or transaction.

## RCNLD is the pipeline baseline described by the plan

Physical page 56 says a value is calculated for all pipelines using **Replacement Cost New Less Depreciation**, abbreviated RCNLD. It describes replacement cost new as the cost of building a new pipeline of equal utility using current prices. It then names four replacement-cost inputs: **location, length, diameter, and composition**.

The same page describes three forms of depreciation:

1. physical depreciation, associated with the age of the pipeline;
2. functional depreciation or obsolescence; and
3. economic depreciation or obsolescence.

After those deductions, the remainder is the RCNLD cost-approach indicator. The page describes a model, not the input evidence for a named pipeline. This article therefore does not supply a length, diameter, composition, service life, utilization measure, survivor curve, depreciation amount, replacement cost, or resulting indicator.

## Unit value is conditional and system-wide

Physical page 57 says a unit-value model **may also be used** for pipelines with appropriate income statements and balance sheets. It says this model is generally used for pipelines that regulation treats as common carriers and that the calculation must cover the entire pipeline system.

The plan then describes several possible indicator layers:

- an income approach using projected typical net operating income and a cost of capital;
- a rate-base cost approach using the stated regulatory investment and book-depreciation frame;
- a calculation for economic obsolescence; and
- a possible stock-and-debt approach in place of a market-data approach.

The word **may** and the data condition matter. The passage does not establish that a named pipeline is a common carrier, that a financial statement is appropriate, that one indicator was selected, or that any model result is current.

## Reconciliation comes before component allocation

The plan says the unit-value indicators are reconciled into a final unit appraisal model indicator. That result is then reconciled with the RCNLD indicator for the entire pipeline system. Only after that whole-system step does the passage say the final correlated value can be allocated among system components to determine a tax-roll value for each segment.

That sequence creates an evidence ladder:

| Evidence layer                 | Record needed to verify it for a named pipeline                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Model eligibility              | The property type, regulatory status, and stated financial-data condition.            |
| Input selection                | The dated source for each cost, income, rate-base, debt, equity, or obsolescence item. |
| Whole-system reconciliation    | The property-specific appraisal work showing the indicators and weighting.            |
| Component allocation           | The stated allocation method and component schedule.                                  |
| Tax-roll result                | The correct appraisal-roll account, year, jurisdiction, and current official record.  |

Stop when one layer is missing. A general plan description cannot substitute for the property-specific record at the next layer.

## Land is separated under stated plan rules

Physical page 58 says land valuation for utility and pipeline properties is the responsibility of appraisal-district staff. It separately says railroad corridor land is included in the appraisal of operating property. Those statements should not be silently merged.

The page also says the rate-base cost, stock-and-debt, and income approach models must be reduced by the value of land to reach a value for improvements, personal property, and other operating property. That is a model-component separation rule. It does not identify who owns land, establish the area or boundary of an easement, interpret a right-of-way instrument, set compensation, or determine a surface or mineral value.

For title, inspection, sketch, and hidden-condition assumptions in the different industrial section, use [Pecos CAD Industrial Appraisal Limits: Title, Inspection, and Sketches](/blog/pecos-cad-industrial-appraisal-limits-title-inspection-and-sketches/).

## Infrequent mixed-property sales limit what review can show

Physical page 59 says appraisal-to-sales ratios are the preferred performance measure but sales are very infrequent. It adds that market transactions normally involve multiple sites and include real and personal property plus tangible and intangible property, making analysis difficult and subjective.

That is a source limitation, not a conclusion that no relevant sale exists. It also does not validate one comparison, one allocation, one model, or one appraisal. The plan says performance is also assessed through valid single-property appraisals submitted for staff review and annual testing by the Texas Comptroller's Property Tax Assistance Division.

The [Texas Comptroller Property Tax System Basics page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/basics.php) explains the appraisal district's local role and January 1 timing. The [Comptroller Valuing Property page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/valuing-property.php) supplies statewide context for market value and generally accepted appraisal methods. Neither source validates a Pecos pipeline account, model choice, input, reconciliation, allocation, land value, or tax result.

## A five-field pipeline-model source worksheet

When a pipeline appraisal statement appears in a record, preserve these fields before drawing a conclusion:

| Field                    | Record exactly                                                                 | Stop condition                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Property scope           | Pipeline, utility, railroad, land, personal property, improvement, or mineral. | Stop if categories are being mixed.                                           |
| Effective date and year  | The stated January 1 date, appraisal year, and source version.                 | Stop if a current conclusion is being drawn from an unmatched year.           |
| Model and condition      | RCNLD, unit value, or another indicator, plus any stated eligibility condition. | Stop if a conditional method is presented as automatic.                       |
| Reconciliation level     | Asset, component, segment, or entire system.                                   | Stop if a whole-system result is presented as a segment result without allocation evidence. |
| Land treatment           | Who handles land and which indicators are reduced by land value.               | Stop if model separation is presented as ownership or easement evidence.      |

This worksheet preserves source structure. It does not select an appraisal method, validate a model, determine tax, interpret a deed or easement, or calculate a private value.
