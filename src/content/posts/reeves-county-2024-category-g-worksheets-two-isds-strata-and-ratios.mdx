> **Public worksheet guide, not a mineral valuation.** This article identifies the current official route to the two Reeves County 2024 Category G worksheets and explains their displayed structure. It reproduces no property identifier or property-specific value. It does not establish ownership, acreage, a property connection, appraisal-roll status, tax, protest rights, legal effect, market value, a private mineral-interest value, an offer, or a transaction result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the Texas Comptroller's Reeves County 2024 final-findings page routes to two separate Category G worksheets: one for Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD and one for Balmorhea ISD**. Both pages display G1 strata 2 through 5, property-row labels, stratum totals, and a Category G totals table. Read them as two dated school-district study records, not as one countywide owner statement or a private mineral valuation.

## Direct answer: keep five identity fields attached

| Identity field | Record before reading the table | Stop before inferring |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Findings year | `2024 Final Findings` | That the page is the newest overall PVS release |
| County | `195 Reeves County` | A connection to a particular owner or tract |
| School district | Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD or Balmorhea ISD | That the two worksheet rows may be merged |
| Category and stratum | Category G, G1, and the displayed stratum | A private mineral classification or value |
| Aggregation level | Property row, stratum total, or category total | That a total is a property-specific result |

The [School District Property Value Study overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/pvs/) explains that the PVS estimates school-district taxable property value and supports the distribution of state education funding. That purpose is the first boundary. A PVS worksheet is not an MRX offer model, a reserve report, a deed analysis, or a private mineral appraisal.

## Resolve the 2025-versus-2024 date question first

The [2025 PVS Final Findings overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/pvs/2025f/index.php) says the Comptroller certified the 2025 findings on **August 27, 2026**. The [Reeves County 2025 final-findings index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2025F/195index.php) currently identifies the same two school districts but exposes only a School District Summary Worksheet and Deduction Detail under each one. It does not expose a Minerals Category Worksheet link.

The [Reeves County 2024 final-findings index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2024F/195index.php) has a different link inventory. Under each school district, it includes a Minerals Category Worksheet in addition to summary, deduction, confidence-interval, stratified-ratio, field-study, productivity, and utilities routes.

That is why this article's title says **2024**. It describes the posted 2024 Category G worksheet pair. It does not say the 2024 PVS is the newest release or that the absence of a Category G link on the current Reeves 2025 page proves anything about an owner, property, category, appraisal, or tax result.

## Follow the county index into two separate district pages

The 2024 county index separates:

1. `195-901 Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD`, labeled as a split district; and
2. `195-902 Balmorhea ISD`.

Each district has its own Category G route:

- [2024 Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD Category G Worksheet](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2024F/1951959011K.php)
- [2024 Balmorhea ISD Category G Worksheet](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2024F/1951959021K.php)

Do not copy a row or total from one page into the other. The district label, county, findings year, category, stratum, and aggregation level form the source identity.

## Both pages display the same three structural layers

The two worksheets share a visible hierarchy:

| Layer | Labels visible on the official pages | What the layer does not establish |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Property rows | `Property ID`, `Local Value`, `PTAD Value`, `Ratio` | Owner identity, title, acreage, a private appraisal, or offer value |
| Stratum sections | `CATEGORY G1 - STRATUM 2` through `STRATUM 5`, followed by stratum totals | That one stratum applies to an owner or that a row can be generalized to another property |
| Category totals | `Sample Parcels`, `Stratum Parcels`, sample and stratum value fields, `Stratum Ratio`, `Category Ratio` | A tax bill, market trend, reserve value, sale price, or recommendation |

This article names the labels so a reader can orient within the official page. It intentionally omits the displayed property IDs and property-specific values. To evaluate a particular public record, use an authorized controlled workspace and verify the exact page, row, year, district, and official context.

## Read sample fields and stratum fields as different evidence levels

The Category G totals table separates **sample** fields from **stratum** fields. Its headings include Sample Parcels, Stratum Parcels, Sample Local Value, Sample PTAD Value, Stratum Local Value, Stratum Ratio, Stratum PTAD Value, and Category Ratio.

Those labels should not be flattened into a single number. A sample-property row, a stratum total, a modeled stratum field, and a category ratio occupy different evidence levels. Preserve the heading attached to every observation. If a copied figure loses its heading, district, findings year, or aggregation level, stop and return to the official page.

The worksheet also displays a `Comp Code` column in its Category G totals area. This article records the label only. It does not decode a code, apply a ratio, recalculate a value, or decide whether a sampling or comparison method is appropriate.

## A ratio label is not a private value conclusion

The official pages place ratio labels beside local and PTAD value fields. That structure belongs to the PVS and its school-district taxable-value purpose. It does not answer:

- who owns a mineral interest;
- whether a property row matches a deed, division order, lease, well, or owner;
- what acreage or decimal is correct;
- what a current appraisal-roll account states;
- what tax is due or whether an exemption applies;
- whether a protest is available or advisable;
- what reserves, production, commodity assumptions, or discount rate apply; or
- what a buyer should offer for a private mineral interest.

For the statewide definition boundary among G1, G2, and G3, use [Texas Comptroller Category G1, G2, and G3 Mineral Classification Boundary Table](/blog/texas-comptroller-category-g1-g2-g3-mineral-classification-boundary-table/). For a source-separated owner research log, use [Reeves County Mineral Records: A Source-by-Source Search Log](/blog/reeves-county-mineral-records-search-log/). Those articles own different jobs and should not be merged into the PVS worksheet reading path.
