> **Public school-district study, not a private mineral appraisal.** This article reports bounded fields from official Texas Comptroller PVS pages. It does not establish title, ownership, acreage, an individual mineral account, appraisal correctness, a tax bill, protest rights, market value, a private mineral-interest value, an offer, or a transaction result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the Reeves County 2025 preliminary PVS index routes to two separate school-district summary worksheets**. The Reeves County portion of Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD shows a Category G figure of **23,324,129,394**; Balmorhea ISD shows **288,604,250**. On each page, that district figure appears in the local tax roll value, PTAD value estimate, and value assigned columns, while the WTD mean ratio is `N/A`. Keep the two rows separate and do not treat either as an owner-level mineral value.

## Direct answer: preserve the district before the number

The shortest reliable citation record has four parts: `2025 preliminary`, `Reeves County`, the exact school district, and `Category G - All Minerals`. If one part is removed, the number becomes easier to misuse.

| District route | Category G local tax roll value | WTD mean ratio | PTAD value estimate | Value assigned |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD, Reeves County portion | 23,324,129,394 | N/A | 23,324,129,394 | 23,324,129,394 |
| Balmorhea ISD, Reeves County | 288,604,250 | N/A | 288,604,250 | 288,604,250 |

This table reproduces the two official rows without publishing a combined total. The rows belong to different school-district routes. Adding them would create a new aggregation that neither source page presents.

## Start with what the PVS is for

The [Texas Comptroller School District Property Value Study overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/pvs/index.php) explains that the study estimates taxable property value for school districts and that the commissioner of education uses the results in distributing public-education funding. That purpose controls how the data should be described.

A PVS summary is not a private mineral appraisal. It does not say what a particular owner holds, where the interest lies, what decimal applies, whether production is attributable to that interest, what burdens attach, or what a buyer might offer. Its public-administration purpose is different from a private transaction question.

## Confirm the preliminary edition and county split

The official [2025 Preliminary Findings](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/pvs/2025p/index.php) page labels the edition `preliminary` and says the information was certified to the commissioner of education on January 30, 2026. It also states that when a school district overlaps two or more counties, the findings are split so that a county presentation includes only property located within that county.

That boundary is especially important for Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD. Its all-splits navigation names both Reeves and Ward counties, while the relevant worksheet section is explicitly labeled `195-Reeves/Reeves County`. A citation should retain that county section instead of describing the displayed row as an undifferentiated district-wide number.

The preliminary page also states a 40-day petition period for a school district or eligible taxpayer. This article does not interpret who qualifies, calculate a deadline, or turn the PVS petition process into an owner-level property-tax protest instruction.

## Use the Reeves index as a routing record

The official [Reeves County 2025 preliminary index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2025P/195index.php) lists two district sections:

1. `195-901 Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD (split district)`; and
2. `195-902 Balmorhea ISD`.

Each section has its own School District Summary Worksheet link and its own Deduction Detail link. The index therefore answers a routing question: which district evidence object should be opened? It does not supply a mineral account, an owner name, or a private-value conclusion.

A practical capture note should record the index URL, the route label clicked, the destination URL, the retrieval time and timezone, and the exact county-and-district heading on the destination page. That creates a checkable path from the county index to the quoted row.

## Read the Pecos-Barstow-Toyah row in place

The [Pecos-Barstow-Toyah ISD 2025 summary worksheet](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2025P/1951959011D.php) presents multiple geographic sections. In the `195-Reeves/Reeves County` section, the Category G - All Minerals row displays:

- local tax roll value: `23,324,129,394`;
- 2025 WTD mean ratio: `N/A`;
- 2025 PTAD value estimate: `23,324,129,394`; and
- 2025 value assigned: `23,324,129,394`.

Near the end of that Reeves County section, the page states that the SDPVS found local value valid and certified local value. Preserve that wording as a school-district study result. It does not certify a named owner's title, mineral acreage, division-order decimal, appraisal notice, tax amount, or private market price.

Do not substitute the page's Ward County section or its all-splits navigation label for the Reeves County row. The repeated district name is not enough; the county subsection is part of the evidence identity.

## Read the Balmorhea row as a separate object

The [Balmorhea ISD 2025 summary worksheet](https://comptroller.texas.gov/auto-data/PT2/PVS/2025P/1951959021D.php) identifies `195-Reeves/Reeves County` and `195-902/Balmorhea ISD`. Its Category G - All Minerals row displays:

- local tax roll value: `288,604,250`;
- 2025 WTD mean ratio: `N/A`;
- 2025 PTAD value estimate: `288,604,250`; and
- 2025 value assigned: `288,604,250`.

The Balmorhea page also states that the PVS found local value valid and certified local value. Treat this as a separate district result. Do not merge it into the Pecos-Barstow-Toyah row merely because both appear under Reeves County.

## Do not translate N/A into zero

Both Category G rows display `N/A` in the WTD mean-ratio column. Report that field exactly as `N/A`. It is not `0`, and this article does not infer a missing-data error, a perfect ratio, a failed ratio, or a private valuation conclusion from it.

The adjacent equality among local tax roll value, PTAD value estimate, and value assigned is also a displayed worksheet fact. It should not be reframed as proof that any individual mineral account is correct. The district-level certification sentence and the owner-level evidence question remain different things.

## Bottom line

The Reeves County 2025 preliminary PVS is not one undifferentiated mineral-value page. It is a county routing index with separate district evidence objects. Preserve the district, county section, preliminary edition, Category G label, displayed columns, `N/A` ratio field, and certification context every time a figure is cited.
