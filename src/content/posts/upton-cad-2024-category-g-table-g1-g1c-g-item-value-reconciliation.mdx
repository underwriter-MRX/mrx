> **Official-report arithmetic, not a property valuation.** This page reconciles three aggregate rows in one Upton County Appraisal District annual report. It does not identify a person or property; define a local code suffix; establish title, ownership, acreage, production, reserves, appraisal, taxable value, tax owed, current market value, mineral-rights sale value, offer quality, legal effect, or a transaction result; or recommend an action. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the Upton CAD 2024 G1 row plus the G1C row equals the G* subtotal across every populated value column used in this comparison.** On physical page 7, the item check is `245,023 + 25 = 245,048`; the Mineral and Total Market Taxable check is `13,018,457,820 + 15,906,260 = 13,034,364,080`; and the Total Net Taxable check is `12,739,319,758 + 14,053,644 = 12,753,373,402`.

That is a table reconciliation, not a countywide mineral-value shortcut. The same [official Upton CAD 2024 Annual Report](https://uptoncad.org/Forms/GetDocument?subPath=Data%20Records&fileName=1731513258_2024%20Annual%20Report.pdf) separately reports **Total Mineral Market Value** as **258,233 items** and **16,516,807,470** on physical page 5. G* and that report recap are different rows with different totals.

## Keep statewide definitions and local codes in separate lanes

The [Texas Comptroller Property Classification Guide](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/docs/96-313.pdf) supplies a statewide classification boundary: Category G1 includes oil and gas interests. The existing [Texas G1, G2, and G3 boundary table](/blog/texas-comptroller-category-g1-g2-g3-mineral-classification-boundary-table/) owns the detailed statewide comparison.

The Upton annual-report table also shows `G1C`. Article 305 does not assign a meaning to that local suffix because the cited statewide page does not define it and the annual-report row alone is not enough to invent one. Preserving an unresolved code is more accurate than filling the gap with an analogy from another county or dataset.

## Do not interchange Items with other units

The annual report uses the visible heading `Items`. Keep that label. An item count is not automatically a count of unique owners, accounts, mineral interests, wells, leases, parcels, tax bills, or deeds. One person may be connected to more than one record, and one record may reflect relationships that the aggregate table does not show.

The current [Upton CAD Property Search](https://www.uptoncad.org/Home/Search) is an adjacent interface, not evidence that an aggregate row maps one-to-one to its searchable records. This article does not execute a search, retrieve a result, publish a name, or connect the report totals to a property.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do G1 and G1C add to G* in the Upton CAD 2024 report?

Yes. The displayed Items, Mineral, Total Market Taxable, and Total Net Taxable figures each reconcile exactly when G1 and G1C are added.

### Where are the G1, G1C, and G* rows?

They appear on physical PDF page 7, which is internally labeled page 3 of 42 in the table section.

### Is G* the report's Total Mineral Market Value?

No. G* is the subtotal shown in the Category G block on physical page 7. The report separately shows Total Mineral Market Value on physical page 5.

### Does the report explain what G1C means?

The cited table displays the G1C code, but this article does not assign a definition to the local suffix without a separate controlling source.

### Do these totals show what one owner's mineral rights are worth?

No. They are annual-report aggregates. They do not establish an owner, account, title, acreage, property appraisal, tax bill, current market value, mineral-rights sale value, or offer quality.
