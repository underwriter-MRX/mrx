> **Source map, not an account audit.** This page maps four adjacent sections in one Upton County Appraisal District reappraisal plan. It does not prove that every account followed the sections in chronological order; identify a person, account, lease, well, or property; show which source was used for a particular account; validate an appraisal method or result; recommend a protest action; or establish title, ownership, tax, legal effect, private mineral value, an offer, or a transaction result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the reviewed Upton CAD plan moves from source categories to model calibration, then to individual value review, and finally to a stated Comptroller performance test.** Those sections appear on physical pages 21 through 24, which are printed pages 19 through 22 inside the document.

That is a reading sequence, not evidence about one account. The [official Upton CAD 2025-2026 Reappraisal Plan](https://uptoncad.org/Forms/GetDocument?subPath=Data%20Records&fileName=1750881192_REAPPRRAISAL%20PLAN%2025%20%26%2026.pdf) describes general source categories and procedures. It does not disclose which inputs were used for a reader's property, whether an input was complete or corrected, or whether a resulting value was accurate.

## Stage 1: identify source categories without claiming account use

Physical page 21 / printed page 19 says historical production, price, and expense data are collected from various sources and entered into a computer system. It names state agencies and commercial data firms as examples and says property characteristics drive a computer-assisted mass-appraisal approach.

Physical page 23 / printed page 21 supplies more source categories. It names Railroad Commission data as reported by operators, updated well and production files, fieldwork, operator financial data, chief appraisers, tax assessors, trade publications, local news sources, public information, pipeline purchasers or operators for ownership information, taxpayer-filed deeds, and correspondence.

| Claim ID | Source statement preserved | Inference stop |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| C306-01 | The plan lists multiple government, commercial, operator, taxpayer, and public source categories. | Do not say a listed source was used for a particular account. |
| C306-02 | The plan describes electronic and field collection plus account-specific file records. | Do not publish a record or infer completeness, ownership, or accuracy. |

The word `source` does not mean `verified fact`. A source can be outdated, incomplete, corrected later, mapped to the wrong period, or unsuitable for a specific conclusion. The article therefore records the plan's categories without validating the underlying data.

## Stage 3: separate individual review from protest rights

The `Individual Value Review Procedures` section on physical page 23 / printed page 21 says individual property values are reviewed several times. It describes generated benchmark indicators, appraiser review, taxpayer opportunities to review appraised values, operator meetings, and the normal protest and Appraisal Review Board process.

That paragraph combines several review contexts. Keep them separate.

## Stage 4: preserve the plan's test label, then check current terminology

Physical page 24 / printed page 22 says the State Comptroller conducts an independent appraisal-performance test through an annual `Property Value Study` for school-funding purposes. The paragraph mentions uniformity, median level of appraisal, coefficient of dispersion, preliminary appeals, and a published report.

The plan was printed in July 2024. Current Comptroller pages use more specific labels. The [Property Tax Assistance Division overview](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/about.php) distinguishes the **School District Property Value Study (SDPVS)** from the **Appraisal District Ratio Study (ADRS)**. The [current reports page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/reports/) describes ADRS as measuring appraisal-district uniformity and median appraisal levels and lists median, coefficient-of-dispersion, and other published measures.

| Claim ID | Terminology layer | Safe conclusion |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| C306-08 | July 2024 Upton plan | The plan uses a Property Value Study label in its performance-test section. |
| C306-09 | Current Comptroller overview | SDPVS and ADRS are separate studies with different purposes. |
| C306-10 | Current Comptroller reports page | ADRS reporting includes median level, coefficient of dispersion, and other measures. |

Do not silently relabel the older paragraph as only SDPVS or only ADRS. The better source-control response is to preserve the plan's wording, show the current distinction, and stop before a current Upton result unless a separately verified current result is in scope.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Upton CAD mineral appraisal review chain?

It is a reading map for four adjacent plan sections: sources of data, model calibration, individual value review, and the plan's stated performance test. It is not an account audit or proof of chronological execution.

### Where are the four sections in the PDF?

They run from physical pages 21 through 24, corresponding to printed pages 19 through 22.

### Does the plan say which source was used for a particular account?

The cited pages list source categories and general procedures. This article does not claim that a particular source was used for a particular account.

### Are PVS, SDPVS, and ADRS interchangeable labels?

No. This article preserves the plan's Property Value Study wording while using current Comptroller pages to distinguish the School District Property Value Study from the Appraisal District Ratio Study.

### Does the plan determine a private mineral-rights sale value?

No. It is a tax-appraisal plan. This article does not calculate or validate a private sale value, offer, tax result, or transaction outcome.

## Bottom line

The citation-ready answer is bounded: physical pages 21 through 24 move from general source categories to calibration, individual review, and a stated Comptroller performance test. Preserve the plan's section order, print date, page locators, exact source identity, and PVS wording. Then keep that older wording separate from current Comptroller SDPVS and ADRS terminology.
