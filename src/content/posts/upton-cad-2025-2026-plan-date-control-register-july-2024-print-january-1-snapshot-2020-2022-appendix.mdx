> **Date register, not an account timeline.** This page separates date labels inside one Upton County Appraisal District plan. It does not identify a person, account, lease, well, or property; prove when an account was appraised or changed; supply a notice or protest deadline; validate an appraisal; or establish title, ownership, taxable status, tax, legal effect, private mineral value, an offer, or a transaction result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the reviewed Upton CAD file contains four date layers, and each has a different role.** The outer plan is labeled for **2025-2026** and says it was printed in **July 2024**. An embedded older report uses a **January 1** effective-date snapshot and states a **2020-2022** applicability frame. Do not collapse those labels into one timeline.

The [official Upton CAD 2025-2026 Reappraisal Plan](https://uptoncad.org/Forms/GetDocument?subPath=Data%20Records&fileName=1750881192_REAPPRRAISAL%20PLAN%2025%20%26%2026.pdf) is the controlled source object. The [Upton CAD Data and Records page](https://uptoncad.org/home/DataRecords) is its current publisher route and displays the plan link. Publisher provenance does not make every embedded page current or prove how a particular account was handled.

## Four date layers, four source-control questions

| Layer              | Observed label | What it answers                                                                | What it does not answer                            |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Outer coverage     | 2025-2026      | Which years the plan says it covers                                            | Whether a procedure occurred for one account       |
| Print state        | July 2024      | When the outer plan says this version was printed                              | An appraisal, notice, protest, or transaction date |
| Appraisal snapshot | January 1      | The effective-date concept described in the appendix and current state sources | When later analysis, notice, or review occurred    |
| Embedded appendix  | 2020-2022      | The older report's stated applicability frame                                  | Current 2025-2026 procedure or account evidence    |

This register resolves a common document-reading problem: a file can have a planning period, a print date, a legally relevant valuation date, and an older embedded report without those dates being interchangeable.

## Layer 3: January 1 is the appraisal snapshot

Physical page 56 of the 94-page PDF, embedded printed page 26, describes January 1 as the effective date for the appraisal and explains that the work may be completed months later. Physical page 57 / embedded printed page 27 describes lien-date value as a snapshot in time.

Current official sources independently preserve the general rule. The [Texas Comptroller's Valuing Property page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/valuing-property.php) says that, with limited exceptions, taxable property is appraised at market value as of January 1 under [Texas Tax Code Section 23.01](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?artSec=23.01&chapter=TX.23&code=TX&tab=1). The [Property Tax System Basics page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/basics.php) says appraisal districts appraise property value as of January 1 each year.

| Claim ID | Evidence layer                     | Safe conclusion                                                                   |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C307-05  | Older embedded Upton report        | It describes a January 1 effective-date snapshot.                                 |
| C307-06  | Current Texas Comptroller guidance | The general appraisal-date rule remains January 1.                                |
| C307-07  | Current official statute route     | Section 23.01 is the cited governing reference for the general market-value date. |

The January 1 snapshot does not tell a reader when source data was downloaded, when calculations were run, when a notice was mailed, or when an owner received a document. It also does not determine a private mineral-rights sale value. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Upton CAD plan date-control register?

It is a source-reading map that keeps the plan's coverage years, print date, appraisal snapshot, and embedded appendix period separate. It is not an account timeline or valuation.

### Does July 2024 mean the plan appraised every property in July?

No. July 2024 is the print label observed in the reviewed plan. It does not prove when a particular account was appraised, reviewed, noticed, or changed.

### Why is January 1 in the register?

The embedded report describes a January 1 effective-date snapshot, and current Texas Comptroller pages independently describe January 1 as the general annual appraisal date.

### Is the 2020-2022 appendix current evidence for 2025 or 2026?

No. Its older applicability frame must remain visible. It cannot be silently treated as proof of current procedures or a current account result.

### Does this register provide a tax, protest, or sale deadline?

No. It does not calculate or advise on a deadline, tax, protest, private mineral value, offer, or transaction timing.

## Bottom line

The citation-ready answer has four columns: **2025-2026 is the outer plan's coverage label; July 2024 is its visible print state; January 1 is the appraisal snapshot described in the appendix and current official guidance; and 2020-2022 is the embedded report's older applicability frame.** Keeping those roles separate prevents an old appendix, print label, or effective date from becoming an unsupported current account claim.
