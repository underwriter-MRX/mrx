> **Source map, not an interpretation of law or a live request decision.** This article separates statements in one Upton County Appraisal District policy scan from a dated 2026 Texas Attorney General verification layer. It does not submit a request, calculate a fee, set a deadline, identify a person or property, or decide compliance. Consult qualified counsel for an interpretation of law. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction. When that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **read the Upton CAD open records policy through two evidence layers and four distinct states**. First, record exactly what the district's scanned policy says about the **request**, **initial response**, **clarification**, and **charge or estimate**. Second, check each statement against the separately dated 2026 Texas Attorney General materials. When the layers differ, preserve the difference and ask the district or OAG; do not silently choose a rule, calculate a fee, or set a deadline for a live request.

That separation matters because the reviewed two-page [Upton CAD policy PDF](https://uptoncad.org/Forms/GetDocument?fileName=1749827956_open+records+request.pdf&subPath=Data+Records) has no visible adoption, effective, or revision date. The [Texas OAG request page](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/members-public/how-request-public-information) is a current public-facing route, and the [2026 Public Information Act Handbook](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/sites/default/files/files/divisions/open-government/PIA-Handbook-2026.pdf) supplies a dated statewide reference. Their roles are different.

## State 1: define a written request for existing information

The Upton scan says formal requests must be written and includes fax and email among its listed routes. The current OAG layer says a request should identify **existing information** and recognizes delivery methods under Texas Government Code section 552.234, including mail, email, hand delivery, and another method approved by the governmental body. Fax or a web-submission route belongs in that approved-method check; it should not be assumed from an old form alone.

A privacy-safe preparation row can contain:

| Field | Record before sending |
| ----- | --------------------- |
| `publisher` | Exact governmental body |
| `requested_object` | Existing record or clearly bounded category |
| `date_range_or_version` | If the source has editions or periods |
| `delivery_route` | Current route confirmed on the official site |
| `format_preference` | Inspection, electronic copy, or other requested form |
| `online_check` | URL and date checked before requesting a duplicate |
| `scope_exclusions` | Personal data and unrelated records not needed for the task |

Do not ask the district to create new information, perform legal research, answer a hypothetical, value minerals, or determine title. The OAG handbook separates requests for existing information from those tasks.

For a mineral-record request, use source identifiers only when necessary and already known through an authorized task. Do not publish a private owner's name, address, account detail, lease detail, or property result in a checklist. The [Howard CAD mineral-record request-form inventory](/blog/howard-cad-open-records-request-form-mineral-record-field-inventory/) owns the separate job of preparing blank request fields.

## State 2: classify the initial response without overreading it

The Upton policy scan describes five initial paths. In source-bound language, the district may:

1. Provide copies of responsive public information.
2. Direct the requester to information on its website.
3. Say it does not hold the requested information or that the material is confidential.
4. Provide or make available what it holds while supplying inspection, copy, or estimated-fee information.
5. Continue searching when custody is uncertain and later respond.

Record the actual response as received. Do not convert a category label into a conclusion that the response is complete, the information is legally confidential, an exception was properly invoked, a search was adequate, or a deadline was met. Those questions require the actual correspondence and current law.

The current OAG materials separately describe prompt production of public information and procedures for seeking an Attorney General ruling when a governmental body believes an exception applies. Article 304 does not claim that the local five-path list and every current statewide procedure are exact equivalents.

## State 3: preserve the clarification loop

The Upton scan describes written clarification when scope is uncertain. It also contains a local statement about treating a request as withdrawn after no written clarification response within 61 days. Keep that number attached to the undated local scan. Do not promote it into a universal or current deadline.

Use this clarification register:

| Field | Preserve |
| ----- | -------- |
| `original_request` | Exact text and delivery evidence |
| `clarification_notice` | Exact notice and date received |
| `scope_question` | The specific ambiguity the district identified |
| `requester_response` | Exact response, if any, and date sent |
| `local_policy_statement` | Wording observed in the Upton scan |
| `current_rule_check` | OAG handbook section or current official guidance consulted |
| `status` | Open, clarified, withdrawn by confirmed authority, fulfilled, or unresolved |

Never silently rewrite the original request, assume clarification was received, or label a request withdrawn solely because this guide contains a number. A real dispute about timing or withdrawal belongs with Upton CAD or the OAG.

## State 4: separate local charge statements from the current estimate layer

The Upton scan lists **$20.50 per hour** for labor, **$25** for appraisal or tax rolls on CD, and written notice when an estimate exceeds **$20.50**. Those are observations from the retrieved local scan, not a fee quote for a current request.

The 2026 OAG handbook separately describes a written itemized estimate when estimated charges exceed **$40** and explains response periods within the applicable statutory context. The two layers should not be silently reconciled. The older-looking local numbers do not authorize Article 304 to calculate a bill, and the current statewide threshold does not prove what a particular request will cost.

For a real request, preserve the exact itemized estimate, delivery date, cited authority, response instructions, scope, requested deposit or bond if any, and final invoice. Ask Upton CAD or the OAG when the local scan and current statewide materials appear to conflict.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Upton CAD policy PDF enough to set a live request deadline?

No. The reviewed scan has no visible adoption, effective, or revision date. Preserve its wording as a local-source statement, verify current statewide guidance, and direct a live timing question to Upton CAD or the Texas OAG.

### What initial response states does the reviewed Upton policy describe?

It describes providing copies, directing the requester to an online source, reporting that responsive information is not held or is confidential, providing available material with inspection or copy and fee information, or continuing a search when custody is uncertain. These are source observations, not a decision about a real request.

### What if Upton CAD says a records request needs clarification?

Preserve the original request, clarification notice, exact scope question, response or no-response state, and every date. Do not silently broaden, narrow, withdraw, or restart a real request based on this article.

### Which charge or estimate threshold applies to a live Upton CAD request?

This article does not decide that. The Upton scan contains local dollar and notice statements, while the 2026 OAG handbook describes a different current itemized-estimate threshold and context-dependent response periods. Confirm the current rule with Upton CAD or the OAG before relying on a number.

### Does a Upton CAD records response prove mineral ownership or value?

No. A request, response, appraisal-district record, search field, or charge notice does not replace title evidence or establish appraisal correctness, tax treatment, production, private mineral value, an offer, or a transaction outcome.

## Bottom line

The citation-ready result is a process boundary, not a legal conclusion: record the Upton local policy as one undated source layer; organize request, response, clarification, and charge information as four states; compare each with dated 2026 OAG guidance; and preserve every conflict in a discrepancy register. Stop before a live deadline, fee calculation, compliance decision, person or property identification, or conclusion about title, tax, value, an offer, or a transaction.

For the broader ownership and transaction framework, continue with the [Texas Mineral Rights Guide](/mineral-rights/texas/). Search visibility, answer-engine retrieval, and LLM citations are dated distribution outcomes if later observed; they do not validate a records request, legal interpretation, ownership, or value.
