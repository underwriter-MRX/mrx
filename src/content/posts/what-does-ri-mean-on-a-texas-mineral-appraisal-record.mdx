**`RI` commonly denotes “royalty interest” in the Texas mineral-appraisal sources reviewed here.** [Pritchard & Abbott’s royalty-owner FAQ](https://www.pandai.com/oil_gas_faq) expands the abbreviation in its examples, and page 19 of [Tarrant Appraisal District’s mineral data-exchange appendix](https://www.tad.org/content/rfp/mineral-and-utility-rfq-2025-2029.pdf#page=19) expressly lists `RI` as `Royalty Interest`. A current [Ward County Appraisal District detail](https://wardcad.org/Home/Details?ownershipId=24888&parcelId=500066) displays `Interest Type: RI` beside a separate `Decimal Interest` field. The vendor [lists Ward CAD as a client](https://www.pandai.com/ward_cad), but neither its FAQ nor Tarrant’s contract is a Ward-issued dictionary for that exact field. Confirm a decision-critical Ward code with Ward CAD. The display alone does **not** establish your title or current payable share.

## Read the type, decimal and category as three different fields

The Ward example displays `Category Code: G1`, `Interest Type: RI` and `Decimal Interest` as distinct labels. The public detail does not define its `RI` code or show the private instrument behind an owner’s interest. Its disclaimer calls the page research information and directs verification of legal descriptions and acreage before legal use. [Ward CAD detail](https://wardcad.org/Home/Details?ownershipId=24888&parcelId=500066)

Pritchard & Abbott’s [FAQ](https://www.pandai.com/oil_gas_faq) uses `RI` for royalty interest in its own royalty-owner examples. Tarrant’s [2024 exchange appendix](https://www.tad.org/content/rfp/mineral-and-utility-rfq-2025-2029.pdf#page=19) puts `Owner Interest Type` and `Owner Decimal Interest of Ownership` in different columns and separately labels `G1` as a state-use code in **Tarrant’s contract**. These explain the abbreviation and field separation. They do not authorize importing Tarrant’s decimal definition into Ward’s system or treating a vendor example as Ward’s exact field specification.

- `Interest Type: RI`: preserve the exact type label, district, property URL and year. The label does not identify your controlling deed or legal interest subtype.
- `Decimal Interest`: preserve the number **on your own record** and its year. Keep it private when asking a public question. The appraisal number is not proof of a payor’s current payment decimal or a buyer’s acquisition fraction.
- `Category Code: G1`: preserve it as a separate appraisal-category label. G1 and RI are not interchangeable, and neither proves your title.

The [Texas Railroad Commission](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) says private property-rights, lease and royalty-payment matters are outside its authority. An RRC identifier shown on an appraisal record can help locate regulatory records; it is not an RRC title finding.

## Compare the record without turning it into a title opinion

For **your own** account, save the appraisal district, exact detail URL, year and the field labels. Record only the minimum parcel or lease identifier needed to retrieve the same row later. Do not repost another person’s name, address or sample decimal to ask what `RI` means.

List the separate sources you actually have: recorded deed or reservation, relevant estate or assignment papers, lease and amendments, unit documents, the payor’s division order, and recent royalty statements. Compare the property description, parties, time period and numbers only for the question each source can answer. The [MRX division-order guide](/blog/what-is-a-division-order-and-why-does-it-matter-for-mineral-rights-owners/) explains the payor-document lane; it does not turn a CAD display into the payor’s current ledger.

If a material difference remains, ask the appraisal district what **its** code and year represent and ask the payor about **its** current records. A qualified Texas land or title professional can assess disputed instruments and the legal chain; an accountant may be needed for a payment reconciliation. The [title, lease and ownership learning center](/learning-center/title-lease-ownership/) maps the broader record types and their limits. You can continue that public research without contacting MRX. If you later bring an exception list to an MRX discussion about a possible transaction, MRX can organize the question but does not certify title or a payable decimal.

### Does `RI` mean royalty interest on every Texas appraisal record?

Pritchard & Abbott and Tarrant Appraisal District use that expansion in the cited materials. Ward displays `RI` but its cited detail does not define the code. Confirm the issuing district’s exact meaning when a decision depends on it.

### Is `Interest Type: RI` the same as the decimal beside it?

No. Ward displays a type label and a separate decimal. The cited materials do not establish that Ward’s displayed decimal equals your current payor payment decimal.

### Does `Category Code: G1` prove I own a royalty?

No. It is a separate appraisal-category field, not a person-specific deed chain or title finding.

### Can I use the appraisal decimal to verify my royalty check?

Treat it as a comparison clue only. Preserve the payor’s division order and current statements and compare them with relevant instruments. A CAD number alone does not settle the payment calculation.

### What if the appraisal label or decimal conflicts with my records?

Save the district, year, exact page and field labels. Ask the appraisal district about its own display and the payor about its own ledger. For a material private conclusion, seek qualified Texas land, title or accounting review.
