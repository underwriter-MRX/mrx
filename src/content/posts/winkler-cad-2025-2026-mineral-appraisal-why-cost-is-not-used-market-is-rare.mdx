> **Published method map, not an owner-specific valuation.** This page reports how an official Winkler CAD contractor plan describes three appraisal approaches. It does not perform an appraisal, select a method for an account, test comparable sales, forecast income, determine title or taxability, establish private mineral-rights value, or recommend a transaction. Any later MRX assessment is educational and directional only, not a certified appraisal. This page is not individualized legal, tax, title, accounting, engineering, or investment advice. MRX may be the disclosed direct buyer in a later transaction; that role is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

The direct answer is: **the Winkler CAD 2025-2026 contractor plan says cost is generally not employed for mineral interests because lease acquisition and development costs may bear no relation to present worth. It says a rigorous market approach is rarely employed as a general rule because usable transaction evidence and complete sale conditions are limited. The plan says Pritchard & Abbott, Inc. (P&A) predominantly relies on the income approach, but warns that the result is only as reliable as its inputs.**

Those statements appear on [whole-PDF pages 21-22, printed pages 19-20 of the 2025-2026 Biennial Reappraisal Plan](https://www.winklercad.org/Forms/GetDocument?fileName=1764618216_Biennial-Reappraisal-Plan.PA_.MIUP_.2025-2026.pdf&subPath=Data+Records). They describe the contractor plan's method roles. They do not determine the private-sale value of an owner's interest.

## The three method roles in one table

| Approach | What the plan says | Required reading limit |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Acquisition and development costs may bear no relation to present worth; the approach is not readily applicable to mineral properties; as a general rule, P&A does not employ it for mineral interests. | This is a report-specific method statement, not a universal rule that cost evidence can never matter. |
| Market | The approach uses evidence from actual transactions, but the plan says disclosed oil-and-gas sales data is limited and meaningful analysis requires known and investigated sale conditions; as a general rule, P&A rarely employs a rigorous market approach. | **Rarely** is not **never**, and the paragraph does not prove whether a particular transaction is comparable. |
| Income | The plan says this approach most readily lends itself to mineral-interest appraisal and converts estimated future income to current value through discounting; P&A predominantly relies on it as a general rule. | Predominant reliance is not a guarantee. The plan warns that the conclusion is only as reliable as the inputs. |

The qualifiers are part of the answer. Removing **as a general rule**, **rarely**, **rigorous**, or **predominantly** would turn a bounded source statement into a stronger claim than the plan supports.

## Why cost is generally not employed

Printed page 19 says the cost approach can be difficult to use for oil-and-gas properties because the cost to acquire and develop a lease may bear no relation to its present worth. The plan then says the approach is not readily applicable to mineral properties and, **as a general rule**, P&A does not employ it for mineral interests.

The source connects a method-selection statement to a specific mismatch: historical or incurred cost does not automatically establish present worth. It does not say that every development cost is irrelevant, that no cost information is ever reviewed, or that the statement governs every appraiser and every mineral property.

That distinction matters because **why this contractor plan generally does not employ a method** is different from **whether a particular cost record is accurate, admissible, or useful for another purpose**. The cited passage answers the first question only.

## Why a rigorous market approach is rare

The same discussion describes the market approach as using evidence from actual transactions. It then identifies several evidence constraints:

- the plan says oil-and-gas sales data is seldom disclosed in Texas, leaving insufficient data for meaningful statistical analysis;
- meaningful transaction evidence requires the conditions of a sale to be known and carefully investigated; and
- transactions involving mergers, acquisitions, or multiple assets can make allocation to one oil-and-gas property difficult.

These are the contractor plan's reasons, not an MRX claim that all Texas sales are private, that no comparable evidence ever exists, or that every multi-asset transaction is unusable. Printed page 20 concludes that, **as a general rule**, P&A **rarely** employs a **rigorous** market approach. The three qualifiers prevent the statement from becoming an absolute prohibition.

The plan does not provide a transaction dataset, comparable-sale adjustment, allocation schedule, or owner-specific market analysis in this passage. It therefore cannot support a private-sale estimate merely because the word **market** appears.

## What predominant reliance on income means

Printed page 20 says the income approach most readily lends itself to mineral-interest appraisal. It describes the approach as converting estimated future income into current value through discounting and says P&A predominantly relies on it as a general rule.

The page also supplies its own reliability boundary: a final value conclusion is no better than the reliability of the information used in the analysis. This warning is material. A method label alone does not establish the quality, completeness, date, or correctness of any input.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the Winkler CAD plan say the cost approach is never used?

No. The plan says the cost approach is not readily applicable to mineral properties and that, **as a general rule**, P&A does not employ it for mineral interests. That is narrower than **never**.

### Why does the plan describe the market approach as rare?

The cited discussion points to limited disclosed oil-and-gas transaction data, the need to know and investigate all sale conditions, and complications when transactions include multiple assets. It does not say that every sale is undisclosed.

### Does rare mean the market approach is impossible?

No. The plan says P&A **rarely** employs a **rigorous** market approach **as a general rule**. The passage does not decide whether usable evidence exists for a particular appraisal.

### Does predominant reliance on income guarantee an accurate result?

No. The plan warns that a value conclusion is no better than the reliability of the information used in the analysis.

### Can this article estimate my private mineral-rights value?

No. It explains method roles in one contractor plan and does not supply an owner-specific appraisal, comparable-sales analysis, forecast, transaction price, tax result, or MRX offer.
